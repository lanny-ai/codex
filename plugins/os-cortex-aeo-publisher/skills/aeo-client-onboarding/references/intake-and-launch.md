# Client intake and launch

## Required intake

- Legal brand and public brand name
- Canonical domain and intended answer-hub path
- Audience, urgent problems, and desired transformations
- Offer and one primary CTA
- Approved claims with supporting source-of-truth URLs
- Prohibited claims, regulated topics, and escalation rules
- Named author and qualified reviewer
- Editorial tone, corrections contact, and citation policy
- Existing site, analytics, Search Console, Bing, and API ownership
- Explicit public-publishing authorization and rollback owner

## Account isolation

Use the client's own ChatGPT/Codex account, Sites project, domain, Git project, OAuth grants, analytics properties, API keys, and billing accounts. Do not make the agency the only owner of a client asset. Give the agency only the access required for delivery and support.

## Acceptance sequence

1. Dry-run project preflight.
2. Supervised publication 1: definition or foundational question.
3. Supervised publication 2: practical how-to question.
4. Supervised publication 3: comparison, objection, or diagnostic question.
5. Establish a 25-question measurement baseline with two paraphrases each.
6. Activate the schedule after every hard gate passes.
7. Observe 14 unattended runs; target at least 95% operational success, zero unsupported claims, and zero publications after a failed gate.

## Member edition defaults

For Build Room and Platinum members, default to saved-only or supervised publication until the first three pages pass. They may enable public auto-publishing afterward. This prevents a new user from publishing weak or incorrectly branded content during initial setup.

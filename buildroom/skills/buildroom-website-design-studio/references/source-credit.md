# Source credit

**The workflow.** The four steps (taste, tools, direction, dial) are adapted from a public video by Chase AI, "The NEW Fable 5.2 Design Workflow Everyone Is Stealing" (2026). It credits these ideas:
- a reference mood board instead of describing taste
- a few deliberate tools instead of many skills
- three very distinct takes to react to
- a tweaks bar with sliders and toggles instead of fix-by-prompt

Build Room adds:
- the taste vault inside the member's Obsidian vault, with the confirmed "what you keep choosing" read
- the three-round cap
- a starter set of controls for non-designers
- the accessibility floor for a 50+ audience
- the lock into a reusable Design System and the Business File
- the hand-offs to every other skill that designs
- the go-live check

The video's own scripts, prompts and screens are not reproduced.

**Anti-generic rules.** `anti-generic.md` is Build Room's own wording. It draws on what these public, open-source design skills and write-ups agree on, summarised and not copied:
- Anthropic's frontend-design skill
- Impeccable (pbakaus)
- taste-skill (Leonxlnx, MIT)
- practitioner articles listed in the research notes

None of them is installed or required; members don't need extra design skills.

**Standards and data:**
- WCAG 2.2 (W3C)
- the GOV.UK Design System type scale
- Nielsen Norman Group's research on older users, page attention and photos
- Baymard Institute on line length
- Google and Deloitte on mobile speed
- Unbounce on single calls to action
- the Google Fonts FAQ and the SIL Open Font License 1.1

**Image provider.** Kie.ai, through the Build Room shared media toolkit (`gen.py`, `ledger.py`), adapted from the job ledger in the create-ads-with-agents skill.

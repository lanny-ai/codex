# Where to find taste: the references card

Give the member this card in Phase 1, in plain words. They need 5–15 screenshots. At least one must come from their own field (a competitor, or a business they admire that serves the same people), and three should carry a one-word reaction.

## Where to look

| Place | Cost | Good for | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **One Page Love** (onepagelove.com) | free | whole one-page sites, landing pages | no sign-in needed |
| **Lapa Ninja** (lapa.ninja) | free | landing pages by category | |
| **Land-book** (land-book.com) | free | landing pages, by style and colour | |
| **Godly** (godly.website) | free | bolder, award-style pages | may lean showy; take one idea, not the whole look |
| **Behance** (behance.net) | free | brand work, photography styles | |
| **Awwwards** (awwwards.com) | free | ambitious sites | often far more complex than a member needs |
| **Pinterest** | free (sign-in may be needed) | mood: colours, photo styles, textures | search broad, then narrow (below) |
| **Dribbble** | free to browse; paid tiers for creators | sections, buttons, small details | many shots are concepts, not real sites |
| **Design accounts on X** | free (sign-in may be needed) | what's fresh this month | follow a few web designers; save what makes you stop |
| **Mobbin** | paid, sign-in | app screens | rarely needed for a coaching business |
| **Businesses in the member's own field** | free | what their buyers already see | at least one is required |
| **The member's own past material** | free | what they already liked enough to use | old flyers, a book cover, a slide they liked |

Sign-in rules on Pinterest and X change often, so don't promise either is open without an account.

## Search phrases that work

Start broad, then narrow to the member's world:

1. `landing page design` · `coaching website` · `service business website`
2. `[their field] website`, such as `retirement coach website`, `bookkeeper website`, `wellness practice website`
3. `[a feeling] website`, such as `calm website`, `warm editorial website`, `bold minimal website`
4. A colour or photo style they mentioned: `sage green website`, `black and white photography website`

## How to save them

- **Mac:** Cmd+Shift+4, then drag over the part you like; Cmd+Shift+3 takes the whole screen. Shots save to the Desktop as PNG.
- **Windows:** Windows+Shift+S, then drag. Paste into Paint and save as PNG, or use the Snipping Tool's save button.
- **Phone:** take a screenshot and AirDrop or email it to yourself.
- Drop them into `Design/Taste Vault/inbox/` in your Obsidian vault. A sub-folder (`inbox/heroes/`, `inbox/colours/`) becomes a collection.
- A whole page, one section, a photo, a colour you like: all count.

## Inspiration, not copying (plain words, not legal advice)

- **Ideas, layouts and methods aren't protected; the actual photos, words, logos and code are** [practitioner, US copyright summary]. A distinctive overall look can also be protected as trade dress.
- So the rule is: **take qualities, never assets.** "Lots of white space and one big photo" is a quality. Their photo is an asset.
- Every direction mixes at least **three** references, so no page is a copy of one site.
- **References are for mood only.** Nothing from the taste vault (an image, a logo, a line of copy) ever goes on the member's page. `design_check.py` refuses a page that uses a vault image, even under a new file name.
- Keeping screenshots privately for inspiration is common practice; whether that's always legally safe is **unconfirmed**. Never publish or share the vault.

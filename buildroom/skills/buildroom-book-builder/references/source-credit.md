# Source credit

**The process.** This skill is the manuscript half of the AI Momentum Labs book engine, adapted for a guided session:
- the fifteen-question author intake
- the outline (title, promise, voice, thread, a brief per chapter)
- drafting in passes with section markers
- the developmental memo and style guide
- the sample-chapter edit with the author's decision
- the section-by-section line edit

The prompts in `outline.md`, `drafting.md` and `editing.md` are that engine's own.

**The review and test-reader process.** The intake sufficiency gate, the independent developmental review, the source, fact and permission audit, simulated and human test readers, the issue register with P0 to P3 severity, controlled revision, the independent copyedit and the regression check come from the engine's operating manual (2026-11-14), where they are written as the proposed procedure with prompts P1 to P10. This skill is where they are first run as real steps.

**What this skill adds:**
- the Business File answers most of the intake
- the member approves the outline
- one chapter is drafted at a time
- a book record carries what earlier chapters established
- the memo's changes are tracked in the register until fixed
- a script gate checks the text and every stage

**Outside research** (editing order, AI-writing tells, readability, test readers, the limits of simulated readers, permissions, Amazon's AI declaration and US copyright) is in `research_notes/Book Builder/outside_research_2026.md` in the Build Room repository, with sources tagged and unconfirmed items marked.

The author-questionnaire approach to planning a non-fiction book is a long-standing method in the field and isn't original to this skill.

# After the manuscript

This skill stops at a tested, publish-ready manuscript. It doesn't typeset, design a cover, write the listing or publish. Here's what the member gets, and what to tell them about the next steps.

## What the member takes away
- `manuscript.docx`, the final edition, exported with `manuscript.py export --edition <final> --docx …`.
- The final edition's plain text and its checksum, so anyone formatting it can prove nothing changed.
- `check.md` saying GO, the claim ledger, the reader summary and the closed issue register.
- The book record and the style guide, for whoever edits or formats it next.

## What's still to do, outside this skill
- **Typesetting** the print interior and the ebook, and **validating** the ebook file.
- **The cover**, checked for exact wording and at thumbnail size. The Website Design Studio's locked colours and fonts (§22) are the starting point.
- **A proofread of the formatted files** (the final-proof prompt P10 in `review-prompts.md`), and a printed proof copy before a print release. A proofreader doesn't replace a subject expert.
- **The listing:** description, keywords, categories, price, author biography.
- **Publishing**, through the member's own account.
- **The launch**, which the Event Launcher plans, and a sample chapter as a lead magnet through Lead Capture.

AI Momentum Labs offers a done-for-you service for this half. Mention it once if the member asks how to get the book produced; don't sell it.

## Two things to say plainly, once
Both are plain-words summaries, not legal advice, and both were current to late 2026 from secondary sources. The member should check the live pages.

1. **Amazon KDP asks authors to declare AI-generated content** (text, images or translations) when they publish. AI-assisted content, where the author wrote it and used AI to edit or brainstorm, need not be declared. The declaration isn't shown to buyers. A book drafted in this skill is most likely "AI-generated" under KDP's wording, even after the author's edits, so the safe answer is to declare it.
2. **US copyright protects human authorship.** Text produced by AI from a prompt alone isn't protected. The author's own writing, their selection and arrangement, and their rewriting are. The more of the book is the member's own words, stories and revisions, the stronger their claim, which is one more reason the skill works from their material and asks them to revise.

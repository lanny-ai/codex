## Adapted from

**`create-ads-with-agents` (Higgsfield edition, v2.1.2)** — an audience-first, multi-agent ad workflow skill supplied to the Build Room founder in October 2026. The Ad Studio adapts, with changes:

- the specialist roles and the separate Realism and Anti-Slop Reviewer (here: the separate reviewer in `quality-gates.md`);
- the evidence discipline: claim statuses, "claims include pictures," the missing-review blueprint rule (`compliance.md`, `service-portfolio.md`);
- concept family vs variant and the fact / guidance / hypothesis split for Meta (`concept-matrix.md`, `ad-principles.md`);
- QUEST for services and the three service routes (`service-portfolio.md`);
- the image direction, worked-example method and typography rules, themselves drawn from that skill author's CINEIMAGE v2.7 production principles (`image-direction.md`, `typography.md`);
- the paid-job ledger: reserve, submitting, record, reconcile, cap, two-repair ceiling (`scripts/ledger.py`, adapted from its `workflow_state.py`).

Changed for the Build Room: service businesses instead of e-commerce; the member's real face and proof as the hero; a provider router (Higgsfield, Kie.ai) instead of a single locked provider; audience read from the Business File instead of researched fresh each time; one concept-table approval before money is spent; copy through the Kern and Halbert systems; deterministic typography as a first-class route for lo-fi formats.

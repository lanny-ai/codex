PROMPT 2 — PROBLEM-AGITATION-SOLUTION SECTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Write the complete emotional core of the page. This is where the
purchase decision is made. Three sections, in sequence:

[SECTION: PROBLEM]
4-6 sentences. Name the problem in plain, specific language.
Mirror the ICA's functional pain precisely.
No selling. No hint of what comes next. Just honest recognition.
This section ends with the reader nodding, not reaching for their wallet.

[SECTION: AGITATION]
5-8 sentences. Three layers:
First: What is this problem costing them? (Loss framing — Kahneman)
       Calculate the cost. Make the math explicit.
Second: What have they already tried that hasn't worked?
        Why didn't it work? (Validates their experience, not stupidity)
Third: What happens if nothing changes?
       Paint the 12-month picture of staying stuck.
Tone: Empathetic but honest. Like a trusted advisor, not a salesperson.

[SECTION: THE PIVOT]
2-3 sentences. No features. No offer reveal.
Just: "It doesn't have to be this way. Here's what changes."
Create anticipation for what comes next without naming it yet.
This is the emotional transition from pain to hope.

TRANSITION LINE:
One sentence that bridges from the problem section to the offer section.
Creates curiosity and momentum. Makes them want to read what comes next.

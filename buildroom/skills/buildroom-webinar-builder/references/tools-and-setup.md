# Tools and setup

Choose the tech for the member: the webinar tool already named in §1 if there is one, otherwise Zoom. Prices change; quote the member's own account page, not a figure from here.

## Zoom Meetings or Zoom Webinars

- **Zoom Meetings** (Pro holds 100 people, Business 300; a Large Meetings add-on raises one meeting to 500 or 1,000) is enough for most members: everyone can see each other if the member wants, chat works, captions are free. The member controls who can unmute and share.
- **Zoom Webinars** (an add-on, sold by attendee tier from 500) adds registration with custom questions, a Q&A panel with upvoting, polls, attendees who cannot unmute or show video, and a **practice session** only the host and panelists see. Worth it for audiences that may grow past a few hundred, or when the member wants the Q&A panel.

## GoHighLevel

GoHighLevel builds the registration page, the reminders and the follow-up workflows, but does not broadcast live video: the broadcast runs on Zoom or another tool. Registering people straight into a Zoom Webinar from GoHighLevel needs Zapier or Make; with Zoom Meetings, the GoHighLevel confirmation simply carries the join link. The **Decision Machine** builds the workflows; the **Event Launcher** writes their words.

## Settings to turn on (Zoom)

- Cloud recording, **Create audio transcript** and **Save chat messages** (the review reads them).
- **Automatic captions** on for the meeting or webinar; tell people where the CC button is.
- Recording notice on (Zoom's own consent notice), and say it in the first three minutes.
- The waiting room off for a webinar people join at once, or set to admit all.
- Screen sharing for the host only; attendees muted on entry.
- A backup host or co-host (the moderator), and a backup link the moderator can post if the host drops.

## The setup list (in `moderator-sheet.md`, ticked by the member)

1. The meeting or webinar created, with the title and time zone right; the join link in the Event Launcher's `event.json`.
2. The settings above.
3. The deck opened in presenter view, the notes visible only to the member.
4. The offer link, the one-pager link and the backup payment link in the moderator's sheet, each tested on a phone.
5. Lighting in front, the camera at eye level, headphones or a headset for clear sound.

## The test call the day before

Twenty minutes with the moderator: join from the member's computer and a phone, share the slides, start and stop the recording, turn on captions, drop each link in the chat and open it on the phone, check the sound. Anything that fails is fixed or dropped. Zoom Webinars' practice session is for this.

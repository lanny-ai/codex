# Source credit

Whose methods this skill adapts, and how. Methods are credited structures; nobody's scripts or slides are reproduced, and partner course material is never used.

| Source | What the skill uses | How |
|---|---|---|
| **The founder** (Build Room) | Live first; the unflattering origin story; "this may or may not be for you"; segmenting the room with chat questions; doors; an honest deadline; Q&A routed to the right door; the moderator dropping the link; AI review of every recording and a rewritten next run; the "what's on offer" one-pager; don't talk past the close | His own method, from his recordings and notes |
| **Jason Fladlien** | The agreement frame; teach the first step; education measured by behaviour change; the honest close for presenters who dislike selling; avoiding "it's not your fault" | Credited; softened for this audience |
| **Michael Stevenson** | Teaching blocks that close their own loops; scripted Q&A on time, money, confidence and the spouse; "the questions people most often ask"; never pretend a recording is live | Credited; his long format and colour anchoring are not used |
| **Frank Kern** | The six-part content webinar; "so that's how… and if you're serious, here's how I can help"; the three pillars (before, during, after); never fake live; find the winner live, then automate | Credited |
| **Mike Koenigs** | Three lessons; resolve the story quickly; close at about 50 minutes; objections handled inside the lessons | Credited |
| **Mitch Carson** | Ask three times; precision | Credited |
| **Dharma Rose** | The paid web class; full value for the ticket | Credited |
| **Mike Filsaime** | The time-coded replay page; real, consequence-based deadlines | Credited |
| **Alex Cattoni** | The emotional order: understood, safe, empowered, excited | Credited, via a framework note |
| **Data** | ON24 2025 and 2026, Livestorm 2026, Goldcast 2025, Univid (via Zoom), WebinarGeek, WorkCast (via Zoom) | Cited with their samples in `webinar-principles.md` |
| **Rules and standards** | FTC Act Section 5; 16 CFR 465; 16 CFR 255; the FTC dark-patterns staff report; 16 CFR 437 and the 2025 proposal; WCAG 2.2 (SC 1.4.3, 1.2.2, 1.2.4); the W3C's notes on older users | Summarised in `honesty-rules.md` and `older-audience.md`; not legal advice |

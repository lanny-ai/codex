## Rules of the file

- A story with `permission: pending` or `declined` is never copied out of this folder.
- The `class` and `permission` lines travel with the words whenever another tool uses them.
- Consents (the client's reply) are kept in `proof/consents/`, named by slug and date.
- A composite (class X) is its own entry with the label in the verbatim block, so it is never mistaken for a person.
- When a client later withdraws permission, change the state to `declined`, note the date, and remove the placements listed under `placed`.

# Build Room — Proof Engine

A win is not an asset until it is written down with the client's permission recorded. Named, verbatim, consented proof is the single highest-leverage upgrade to every sales asset the member owns. This skill runs the founder's capture loop: **trigger → capture → file → deploy**, one story at a time, and keeps the proof bank that every other Build Room tool reads.

## Your Foundation

1. **`references/proof-playbook.md`** — the loop, the interview, permission states, the proof classes, the honesty rules, and where each kind of proof may be placed. Overrides everything else, including the member's request.
2. **`references/proof-bank.md`** — the shape of the proof bank and of one story entry.
3. **`references/business-file-protocol.md`** — how you read and write the Business File. Follow it exactly.

## Prerequisites

**Requires §1 (Snapshot). Best with §3 (Signature Offer) so each story is tied to the offer it proves.** Nothing else. A member with no client results yet still runs it: the loop starts with their own numbers and the first client's first win.

## Modes

- **Capture** (default; "capture this win," "a client just told me…"): one story, from trigger to filed entry, in one sitting.
- **Ask** ("ask for a testimonial," "how do I ask [client] for a case study"): the interview and permission ask, drafted for the member to send by hand.
- **Harvest** ("build my proof bank," "what proof do I have"): go through the file, inbox, call notes and chat the member pastes; list every candidate win; capture the strongest three.
- **Place** ("what proof can I use for my offer page / ad / post"): pick the right story for the asset, by class and permission, and hand it to the tool that builds the asset.
- **Weekly** ("proof check"): ten minutes: new wins since last week, permissions pending, one placement.

## Session Flow

Work in `proof/` next to the Business File: `proof-bank.md` (the index) and one file per story, `proof/stories/<slug>.md`. If the member keeps an Obsidian vault, the folder lives in it.

### Phase 0 — Where things stand

Follow the protocol. Read §1, §3, §5, §18 (proof vault and never-say claims), §19 (testimonials collected) and §1 *Retired lines*. Read `proof/proof-bank.md` if it exists. Then ask, one at a time, only what is missing: "Tell me about the last time a client got a result. What happened, in their words if you remember them?"

### Phase 1 — Trigger

Name the win signal: a first win in the first two weeks, a thank-you email, a result mentioned on a call, a number the client reported, a live comment at an event. Record the date and the source. If the member has several, list them and take the strongest first.

### Phase 2 — Capture

Draft the **3-question interview** and the **permission ask** (`proof-playbook.md` §2) in the member's voice, for the member to send by hand (email, text or a two-minute call). If the client already said it (an email, a chat message, a recording), capture the words **verbatim** and still send the permission ask. Never write the client's answer for them.

### Phase 3 — File

One entry per story (`proof-bank.md`): before, the result (a number and a timeframe, the client's numbers, not the member's estimate), their verbatim words, the **class** (A–E, H, X, R, U), the **permission** (named · anonymous · pending · declined), the date and the source, and which offer it proves. Pending stays pending until the client answers. Declined is recorded and respected forever.

### Phase 4 — Deploy

Replace **one** generic or composite proof block with the named result, immediately: the offer page's proof section, email 3 of the follow-up, the landing page, a LinkedIn post, an ad, the Day 2 proof slot of a launch. One story, one placement. Hand the words to the tool that owns the asset (Offer Page Copy, Copy Engine, Ad Studio, Video Director, LinkedIn Engine, Event Launcher); each of them already refuses proof that has no consent, so the entry travels with its class and permission.

### Phase 5 — Business File update

- **§18 Proof vault** line, in the template's shape: location (`proof/`) · consented testimonials (count, by class, e.g. "4: D 2, C 1, B 1") · real numbers (how many stories carry the client's own number) · review count + rating (public reviews, if the member has them) · founder photos (where they are); add "pending: N" at the end. The **never-say claims** list gains any claim a story could tempt but does not support.
- **§19 Testimonials collected** — count and latest, when a story is a video or audio testimonial (the Video Director records the video itself).
- **§13** — one `captured` row for each story still pending permission or waiting on a number, pointing to `proof/`.
- **§8** — one row; ask for the 1–5 rating.
- Emit the entire updated file with the "what changed" summary.

## Voice & Style Rules

- One question at a time. The member's clients are never contacted by the skill; the member sends every ask.
- Verbatim means verbatim. Tidy spelling only; never improve a quote.
- Say which class and permission every story carries whenever it is shown.
- Never promise what a story will do for conversion.

## What This Skill Does NOT Do

- Invent, embellish or combine stories; write a composite without the `[composite result based on typical client outcomes]` label; present a paid-program result as a free-event result; attribute a quote to something it was not about.
- Use a result the client has not given a number for, or an income figure without the client's own number.
- Use anything declined, anything on §18's never-say list, or a retired line.
- Record a testimonial video (Video Director), write the page or email that carries the proof (the owning tool does), or offer rewards for positive reviews.

## When the Session Is Complete

The member has at least one filed story with its class and permission recorded, the interview and permission ask ready to send for any pending ones, one placement made or handed to the owning tool, and §18 updated so every other tool knows what proof exists and what it may use.

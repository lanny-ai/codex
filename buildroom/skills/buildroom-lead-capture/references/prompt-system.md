Write the complete landing page for this lead magnet.
Publication-ready. No placeholders. No outlines.
Every word written and ready to paste into a page builder.

Use this exact section format:

[HEADLINE — 5 OPTIONS]:
Write 5 headlines using these structures:
Option 1: Specific outcome + timeframe ("Get X in Y days")
Option 2: ICA mirror ("For [specific person] who [specific situation]")
Option 3: The exact question they're Googling right now
Option 4: The fast win promise ("Finally: [specific thing] without [specific pain]")
Option 5: Pull from their verbatim language (use exact ICA phrases)
Mark your recommended headline and explain why.

[SUBHEADLINE]:
One sentence. Expands the headline. Adds one specific detail
that makes the promise feel real and achievable.

[OPENING HOOK — 2-3 sentences]:
Written directly to the ICA by situation, not demographic.
"If you're a [specific type of person] who [specific situation]..."
Makes them feel this was written for them specifically.

[WHAT YOU GET — 4-5 bullets]:
Each bullet = one specific thing they receive.
Each starts with a verb.
Each describes an outcome, not a feature.
NOT: "A 10-page PDF guide to client acquisition"
BUT: "The exact 3-message sequence that books discovery calls
     from your existing contacts — copy, paste, send"

[WHO THIS IS FOR — 3 bullets]:
"This is for you if..."
Pull from ICA language. Specific situations, not demographics.

[WHO THIS IS NOT FOR — 2 bullets]:
Honest. Filters bad-fit leads. Makes right-fit leads feel
more specifically seen.

[THE FORM SECTION]:
The micro-copy above the form (1-2 sentences):
Tells them exactly what happens after they submit.
"Enter your name and email — I'll send it to your inbox
in the next 2 minutes."
Form fields: Name + Email only. Nothing else.

[CTA BUTTON — 3 options]:
Short. Action + outcome. Not "Submit" or "Download."
Example: "Send Me the [Lead Magnet Name]"

[SOCIAL PROOF]:
What to put below the form:
If you have a testimonial: write the format to use it
If you have a number: "Downloaded by 200+ [ICA type]"
If you have neither: write a credibility statement about
your track record with this specific type of client

[TRUST STATEMENT]:
One line. Under the button.
"No spam. Unsubscribe anytime." or something equivalent
that removes the last micro-objection.

PART A — THE THANK YOU PAGE:

Write the complete thank you page.
This is the highest-engagement moment in the funnel.
The visitor just said yes. They are in a YES state.
Do not waste it with "Thanks! Check your email."

Structure:

[CONFIRMATION HEADLINE]:
Confirms what they did. Creates immediate relief.
"You're in. [Lead Magnet Name] is on its way."

[WHAT HAPPENS NEXT]:
Exact, specific next steps. Sets expectations precisely.
"Check your inbox — it's arriving in the next 2 minutes.
If you don't see it, check your spam folder and mark
[my email] as safe."

[THE NEXT STEP OFFER]:
One natural next step. Not a hard sell. A relevant question.
Options (pick what fits this business):
A) "While you wait — [one-line relevant tip or insight]
   that most [ICA type] never consider..."
   Followed by: a short insight + a soft CTA to book a call
B) A short video (describe what it would say)
C) A direct invitation: "If you want this done for you
   instead of doing it yourself, here's how that works..."
   Followed by: 2-3 sentences on the core offer + a link

[SOCIAL PROOF]:
One testimonial or result here. The visitor is primed to
believe it right now more than at any other point.

# Source credit

**The process.** This skill grows out of the founder's earlier speaking tool, the Gig Getter. Its heart is unchanged: learn the speaker in depth through seventeen questions, asked one at a time, then find where they should speak. Those seventeen questions are in `intake.md`, in the original order.

**What this skill adds to the original:**
- most of the seventeen answers, the talks, the bio and the one-sheet are drafted from the Business File and confirmed by the member, instead of typed in from nothing;
- every stage has a source and a date, so nothing is recommended from memory;
- stages are vetted for fit and for the bad deals aimed at new speakers before any pitch;
- a ranked list, a pitch for each stage, help with call-for-speakers forms, and a check that refuses anything the member can't stand behind;
- a one-page speaker sheet;
- a pipeline with follow-ups, a daily list, a dated plan and a scorecard built from the member's own counts;
- the route from a talk to clients on stages that forbid selling, and the hand-offs to the Webinar Builder, Lead Capture, the Decision Machine, the Proof Engine and the Video Director.

**The original's knowledge base** was a well-known speaker's course on building a speaking career. Ideas carried over from it, in this skill's own words: every talk has one job; one talk that stretches to any slot; cut fifteen points to five; start on free stages where the right people sit; trade instead of discounting; let client stories do the work of a pitch; ask every host and every room for the next one. Left out because they are dated or risky today: the keynote fee ladder and its income claims, tactics for collecting contacts without clear consent, scripted introductions that overstate the speaker, and the statistics it repeats without a source. None of its text is reproduced, and this skill doesn't speak in that speaker's voice or name.

**Outside research** (where speakers are booked, lead times, hosts' published rules on selling, the law on outreach and on claims from the stage, the scams aimed at new speakers, and how little reliable data exists on fees and conversion) is in `research_notes/Speaking Engine/outside_research_2026.md` in the Build Room repository. It was gathered from search summaries in November 2026 and every figure in it is unverified; this skill therefore states none of them as fact.

The pitch patterns follow `kern-framework.md`, which is structure only.

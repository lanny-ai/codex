# Video compliance: G4, consent, labels, music

Not legal advice; the director reviews for these checks and says so. Regulated offers (health, money, housing, employment, legal) check their market's rules and the platform's special ad categories before anything ships. Figures and dates marked † come from search-summary research and should be verified before they are quoted to a customer.

## The rules that shape video

| Rule | What it means for a video | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **FTC Consumer Review and Testimonial Rule (16 CFR 465)** | No fake or AI-generated testimonials, no reviews from people without real experience; civil penalties up to about $53,088 per violation † | Testimonials only from real, consented clients, edited without changing meaning |
| **FTC Endorsement Guides** | Material connections disclosed; typical results not misrepresented | A paid or comped client says so on screen; best-case results carry their context |
| **New York synthetic performer law** (effective June 2026 †) | Conspicuous disclosure when an ad features an AI-created human not based on a real person | No generated person as the hero; any generated person → on-screen "Includes AI-generated imagery" in every ratio |
| **EU AI Act Article 50** (from August 2026 †) | Deepfakes labelled at first exposure | The same label; provenance metadata kept |
| **Platforms** (Meta, YouTube, TikTok) | Disclosure of realistic synthetic content; TikTok reads C2PA; political and social-issue ads have their own rules | Answer each platform's AI questions truthfully at upload; never strip metadata; refuse political and issue videos |
| **Likeness and voice** (state digital-replica and voice laws †) | A face or voice clone needs permission | Written consent before any avatar twin or voice clone, with scope and how to revoke |
| **Music copyright** | A track needs a licence for this use (ads and commercial content often need more than a personal licence) | Licensed library, the platform's commercial library, or generated music with commercial terms; the licence noted in the disclosure sheet |

## G4 checklist (per video)

1. **Every claim and number** (spoken, on screen, or implied by the picture) traces to the proof vault or the Business File, with its source.
2. **Guarantees word for word** from §3, including when the clock starts and what it covers, signed off by the member.
3. **Approximations keep their qualifier** ("about 30 minutes a week").
4. **No synthetic customer, testimonial, rating or result.** A generated scene is never presented as a client's office, work or outcome.
5. **Testimonials verbatim and in context:** cut for length, never for meaning; atypical results labelled; the client's consent covers ads, the name and role shown, and the platforms.
6. **Consent on file** for every recognisable person and for the member's likeness and voice (§19), and for any avatar twin (scope, date, revocation).
7. **AI label** on screen in every ratio whenever a generated person appears; listed in the disclosure sheet for every video with generated footage.
8. **Music licensed** for this use; the licence named.
9. **No deceptive interface:** fake notifications, play buttons, DMs or system alerts.
10. **The destination keeps the promise:** the CTA's offer and terms match the page.
11. **Not political, election or social-issue.**

## The testimonial consent form (proof playbook)

Give it to the client before the interview; keep the signed copy where §19 says:

> I agree that [business] may record an interview with me about my experience and use the recording, my name as "[first name, last initial, role, town]" and my image and voice in its marketing, including paid ads on [platforms], for [period, or until I withdraw]. My words will not be edited to change their meaning. I was / was not given anything in return for this interview ([what]). I can withdraw this consent for future use by writing to [contact]. Signed, dated.

## The avatar-twin consent (the member's own)

Scope (which videos, which platforms), the provider, that the voice and face may be generated saying new lines the member approves, how long, and how to revoke. Recorded in §19 with the date and location of the signed copy. A twin never says a testimonial, never claims to be live, and is never the first video in a series.

## The disclosure sheet

Built by `review_video.py kit`: per video, the generated footage (asset, provider, model), real people and their consent, the AI label, the music and its licence, the claims and their proof, the review verdicts. When uploading: answer the platform's AI-content questions truthfully and keep the original generated files.

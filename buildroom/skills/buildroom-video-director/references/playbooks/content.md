# Playbook: content

**The job:** videos that are useful on their own, build trust and memory with the people who are not buying yet (most of them, most of the time), and grow the audience that later ads and offers reach. Judged on watch time, saves and shares, follows, comments on the keyword CTA, and the retargeting pool.

## Shape

- **Shorts:** 30–90 s, 9:16. One idea each.
- **Long-form:** 4–12 min, 16:9, chapters. One promise each.
- **Structures:** One mistake; Myth vs fact; How I'd do it; Story; Long-form teach (`story-structures.md`).
- **Script:** Kern for shorts; Halbert's story discipline for long-form openings and stories. The ask is soft: save, follow, comment a keyword for a free resource, or the lead magnet from §7. Never a hard sell in every video.

## The series (do this before the first video)

1. **Pillars:** three or four topics from §2 pains and objections and §4 villain, each tied to something the member actually sells.
2. **Titles:** 8–20 per month, each with its hook and structure; a mix of the structures so the feed does not repeat itself.
3. **Cadence the member can keep:** one good video a week beats a daily plan abandoned in week two. Record it in §19 and §14.
4. **Repurposing:** every long-form video yields three to five shorts (its best answers, each with its own hook), cut with the same `edit.json` pattern from the long-form footage.

For many videos a week, see `volume-mode.md` (batch shoot, batch edit, the Video Engine zip).

## Must-haves

- Open on the idea, not on "Hey guys" or a logo sting. First word by 2 s.
- Every claim true and specific; numbers sourced; no invented case studies.
- Captions on every short; chapters and an SRT for long-form.
- The brand cue (face or name) early in every video; the same end card style across the series.
- Value first: the viewer learns something even if they never buy.

## Deliverables

Per video: the final in its ratio(s), captioned, at −14 LUFS, reviewed, approved, with title, description and the keyword or lead-magnet CTA in the publish sheet. Per month: the series plan and the repurposing map.

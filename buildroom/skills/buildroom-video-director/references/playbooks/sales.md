# Playbook: sales

**The job:** a video that moves a warm prospect to the decision: the sales video on an offer page, the offer explainer before a call, or a personalised video to one prospect. Judged on booked calls and closed deals, not views.

## Three formats

| Format | Length · ratio | Structure | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sales video** | 3–15 min · 16:9 | Halbert: situation → the founder's real story → the problem behind the problem → the mechanism → the proof stack → the offer → the guarantee (exact) → real scarcity or none → the ask, twice | Top of the §5 offer page; the email after the opt-in |
| **Offer explainer** | 60–120 s · 16:9 and 9:16 | Who it is for → what we do, step by step → what you get → the price if §3 shows it → the guarantee (exact) → the ask | The booking page, the confirmation email, the reminder sequence (§16) |
| **Personalised prospect video** | 60–90 s · 16:9 (screen + face) | Their name and one specific public fact about their business → the gap noticed → what you'd do first → the ask (reply or book) | One prospect, sent in outreach (§14) or before a call (§15) |

## Script rules

- Halbert for the sales video (it has to carry a cold-ish reader to the order); Kern for the explainer and the prospect video.
- **Guarantees word for word** from §3, including when the clock starts and what it covers, and only after the member has signed off the wording. A shortened guarantee is a new claim.
- **Price and terms** exactly as §3 allows them to be shown.
- **Proof** only from the vault: consented testimonials, real numbers with sources, real screen recordings. A missing proof beat becomes a "what we'll measure together" beat, never an invented one.
- **Scarcity** only if it is real and the number is set (an open [N] is not scarcity).
- The ask said twice in the sales video: once after the offer, once at the end.

## The personalised prospect video

- Public facts only: their website, their public posts, their reviews. Never anything private, scraped from a login, or guessed about their finances or health.
- Screen recording of their site or their public page with the member's face in a corner (record with a free screen recorder), or the member to camera with one insert.
- One specific observation, one suggestion, one ask. Under 90 s.
- Personalised at scale only with real personalisation; never a generated face or voice pretending to be live.

## Shoot

Sales video and explainer: horizontal, MCU, a teleprompter, the real place (`shoot-list.md`); screen recordings of the real process and deliverables for B-roll. The member's twin may make scale variants of the explainer's opening only, with consent.

## Deliverables

The final(s), captioned, at −14 LUFS, chapters for the sales video, a thumbnail, the page embed note (where on §5 it goes), reviewed and approved.

## Business File

§19, the §5 reference (video embedded on the offer page, with the date) or the §15 reference (the explainer or prospect video in the sales process), §13 rows (a landing update, the next prospect batch), §8.

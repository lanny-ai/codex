# Playbook: proof

**The job:** turn a happy client into a consented testimonial video, and its best lines into proof the member can use everywhere (ads, pages, sales videos). Judged on consented testimonials collected. This playbook raises the ceiling on every other one: most members' ads and pages are capped by thin proof.

## Who to ask, and when

- A client who just got a specific result (the §6 Day-14-win or first-result trigger is the moment). Not the member's friend, not a paid actor, never a composite.
- Ask in the member's own words, by message or on a call. Offer the questions in advance and say how long it takes (20 minutes). Disclose anything given in return.

## Consent first

The client signs the testimonial consent form (`video-compliance.md`) before the interview: ads allowed or not, how their name appears, platforms, period, how to withdraw, anything received in return. No signed form, no use in ads.

## The interview (20 minutes, remote or in person)

Remote: a video call recorded in the platform's highest quality, the client on a laptop camera at eye level, facing a window, with earbuds. In person: the shoot list's phone setup, the client in their own place of work.

Questions that draw out specifics (ask, then follow with "what does that look like in numbers?" or "can you give me an example?"):

1. Tell me about your business and who you serve.
2. What was going on before we worked together? What had you tried?
3. What almost stopped you from saying yes? *(the objection; it maps to §2)*
4. What did we actually do, step by step?
5. What changed, and how quickly? What can you measure? *(numbers and timeframes, their words)*
6. What surprised you?
7. What would you say to someone in the situation you were in?

The member asks, then stays quiet. The best lines come after the pause.

## The edit

- **Verbatim and in context.** Cut for length, never for meaning; no reordering that changes what was said; keep the context of a result ("in our first month, with our own list"). Written quotes keep their exact punctuation too (em dashes included); house style never edits a quote. Each result keeps its timeframe and what it was about (training, a build, the product).
- **Structures:** Before · during · after; The objection answered (`story-structures.md`).
- **Cuts:** one 2–3 min story (16:9 for pages and YouTube), three 20–40 s clips (9:16 for ads and social), each built around one specific line.
- Lower-third with the name as consented. Light music or none. Captions burned in.
- A best-case result labelled as such if it is not typical.

## Into the proof vault

Every usable line becomes a §18 proof vault entry: `P-ID · testimonial · "exact words" · name as consented · source: interview YYYY-MM-DD (file, timestamp) · consent: signed YYYY-MM-DD, ads yes/no · video clip file`. The Ad Studio and the sales playbook can only quote what is in the vault.

## Deliverables

The full story and the clips, reviewed and approved by the member and **sent to the client to approve** before publishing (their name is on it); the signed consent filed; the vault entries.

## Business File

§18 proof vault (new entries), §19 (testimonials collected, consent location), §13 rows (the ad or page that should use each new proof), §8.

# Playbook: ad

**The job:** a paid video ad that stops the right person, makes one promise they can believe, and gets one action, judged on cost per qualified lead.

## Where it comes from

Always the **Ad Studio**. The studio has already done the brief (G0), the matrix and the concept (G1), and the copy (G2): angle, hook tactics, primary text, headline, the proof IDs, the destination. The director inherits those gates and the studio's ledger (`ads/YYYY-MM/project.json`), works in `ads/YYYY-MM/video/`, and hands finished videos back into the studio's kit. A member who asks the director for an ad directly is sent to the studio first ("Make me ads"), because an ad without a concept is a guess.

## Shape

- **Length:** 15–45 s. Cold audiences 15–30 s; retargeting can take 45 s with more proof.
- **Ratios:** 9:16 (Reels, Stories, TikTok) and 4:5 (feed). 1:1 only if the member runs LinkedIn.
- **Structures:** Hook → Problem → Proof → Ask; Confession; Us vs them; Offer; Proof first (`story-structures.md`). One per concept.
- **Hooks:** the studio's three hooks become three versions of the opening 2–3 s on the **same body**: shoot all three, cut three variants. That is the test.
- **Script:** Kern. Spoken lines short; the hook is said in the first second; the brand by two seconds.

## Must-haves

- First word at or before 1.0 s (the review fails it otherwise).
- Brand cue by 2 s: the member's face, the logo or the name.
- One proof moment that is real: a client's consented line, a real number with its source, a screen recording of the real deliverable.
- End card with the ad's CTA held at least 2.5 s; it matches the studio's destination and landing headline.
- Captions burned in (SubMagic or the director's), inside the 9:16 safe zone.
- No generated person as the hero; the AI label if any generated person appears.

## B-roll

What the line names: the situation (the desk at 7am, the phone buzzing, the empty calendar, the client's shop with consent), the deliverable, the result on a real screen. Generated scenes only for situations the member cannot film this week. A cutaway every 3–5 s keeps a founder-to-camera ad moving.

## Deliverables

Per concept: three hook variants × two ratios, captioned, at −14 LUFS, through the studio's review and the member's approval, named into the studio's kit (`kit/videos/`) with the publish sheet rows. The studio's test plan covers them: judge on hook rate (3-second views ÷ impressions), hold rate, CTR and cost per qualified lead against the account's own baseline.

The Ad Studio writes §18's current cycle. The director writes §19 (playbook run, footage location, caption template) and §8.

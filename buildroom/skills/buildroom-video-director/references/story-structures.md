# Story structures

Pick one structure per video from the playbook's list. A structure is a sequence of jobs, not a template of words. Every beat must do its job; if a beat has nothing true to say, cut it.

The rules under every structure:

- **Open on the situation, the question or the face** in the first two seconds. Never on a logo animation, a slow reveal or "Hi, I'm…".
- **Brand on screen by two seconds:** the member's face, name, colour or logo.
- **Emotion opens, reason closes:** the felt situation first, then the specific proof and mechanism.
- **One ask,** held at least 2.5 s on an end card that matches the destination.
- **Length follows awareness** (Schwartz): colder audiences get shorter, situation-led videos; warmer audiences can take more proof.

## For ads (15–45 s)

| Structure | Beats | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook → Problem → Proof → Ask** | 0–2 s hook (situation or question) · 2–8 s the problem in their words · 8–20 s one piece of real proof or the mechanism · last 3 s the ask | Problem-aware cold traffic |
| **Confession** | 0–2 s "I used to…" (true, the member's own) · the cost · what changed · the offer · the ask | Founder-led, trust-building |
| **Us vs them** | 0–2 s the old way named · side by side: what they do now / what this does · the ask | Solution-aware, category reframe |
| **Offer** | 0–2 s the offer in one line · what is included · the risk reversal (exact §3 wording) · the ask | Retargeting, product-aware |
| **Proof first** | 0–3 s the client's result in their own words (consented clip) · the founder explains how · the ask | When the vault has a strong testimonial |

## For content (30–90 s shorts; 4–12 min long-form)

| Structure | Beats |
|---|---|
| **One mistake** | the mistake people make (hook) · why it costs them · the fix in one move · a soft ask ("save this", "follow for part 2") |
| **Myth vs fact** | the myth said plainly · why it sounds right · the fact with one specific example · the takeaway |
| **How I'd do it** | the situation · three steps, each shown · the result to expect (no invented numbers) · the soft ask |
| **Story** | a moment (where, when, who) · the tension · what was learned · what the viewer can do today |
| **Long-form teach** | cold open with the payoff · the promise of the video · 3–5 chapters, each a mini-hook and a demonstration · recap · one ask |

Content is value-first: the ask is soft ("save", "follow", "comment the word X") or a lead magnet, never a hard sell in every video.

## For sales (3–15 min sales video; 60–120 s explainer; 60–90 s prospect video)

| Structure | Beats |
|---|---|
| **Sales video (Halbert)** | the starving crowd's situation · the story (the founder's real one) · the problem behind the problem · the mechanism · proof stack (real) · the offer and what's included · the guarantee (exact §3 wording) · the scarcity that is real, or none · the ask, twice |
| **Offer explainer** | who it's for and the situation · what we do, step by step · what you get · what it costs (if §3 shows it) · the guarantee (exact) · the ask |
| **Personalised prospect video** | their name and one specific, public fact about their business · the gap you noticed · what you'd do first · the ask (a reply or a call) — public facts only, never anything private |

## For proof (2–5 min testimonial; 30–60 s cut-downs)

| Structure | Beats |
|---|---|
| **Before · during · after** | who they are and the situation before (their words) · what they were worried about · what we did together · the specific result and timeframe (their words, true) · what they'd tell someone like them |
| **The objection answered** | the doubt they had before buying (an objection from §2) · what changed their mind · what happened next |

Proof edits keep the client's words verbatim and in context: cut for length, never for meaning. Results come with their context ("in our first month, with our own list").

# The shoot list: ten minutes on a phone

The member's real face and voice carry the video. The shoot list makes the ten minutes count. Give it to the member as a checklist they can follow alone.

## Before you press record

- **Phone:** the back camera if someone can hold it; the front camera on a stand if alone. Clean the lens. 4K or 1080p, 30 fps. Shoot **vertical** for ads and shorts, **horizontal** for long-form and sales videos (the edit crops the other ratios).
- **Framing:** eyes about a third down from the top of the frame; head and shoulders (MCU). Leave a little space above the head. The background is real and tidy: their office, their workspace, their shop. No blank wall unless that is their brand.
- **Light:** face a window. Daylight from the front or slightly to one side. Never a window behind them. Turn off overhead lights that make shadows under the eyes.
- **Sound:** the most important part. A quiet room with soft things in it (sofa, curtains). A clip-on mic plugged into the phone if they have one (about $20 makes the biggest difference of anything here). Phone within arm's length if no mic. Turn off fans and notifications.
- **Room tone:** record ten seconds of silence in the same spot. The edit uses it to cover gaps.

## The takes

- Read each line from a **teleprompter** (any free teleprompter app; paste the approved script, one line per screen). Look at the lens, not the screen.
- **Three takes of every hook,** each slightly different in energy. Hooks decide the video.
- **Two takes of every other line.** Pause two seconds between takes; the edit cuts in the gaps.
- **Say it, don't read it.** If a line feels unnatural to say, flag it: the script changes, not the delivery.
- **One task per take** (direct like this): "Look down at the laptop, then up at me, and start the line on the way up." Not "be confident."
- **B-roll the member can grab in two minutes:** their hands on the keyboard, the real deliverable, the place, walking in. Five seconds each, steady.

## File names

`hook_take1.mp4`, `hook_take2.mp4`, `body_take1.mp4` … into `footage/`. Screen recordings into `footage/screen/`.

## Consent the shoot needs

- **The member:** their likeness and voice in this video, recorded once in §19 (yes/no, date, where).
- **Anyone else on camera** (a client, staff, a passer-by who is recognisable): written consent before the video ships. Proof interviews use the consent form in `video-compliance.md`.
- **An avatar twin** (phase 2 of the member's video habit, not the first video): separate written consent for likeness and voice cloning, scope and how to revoke it.

## After the shoot

The director probes every file (resolution, frame rate, loudness), looks at frames from each take, picks the best take of each line with a reason, and asks for a reshoot of any line whose audio is unclear. Bad audio is the one thing an edit cannot fix.

Since last time

What's new, in plain words

1. The Website Design Studio NEW

Most pages AI makes in 2026 look the same: a cream background, a big headline with one word in italics, little labels over every heading. This tool gives yours a look that's your own, and you show your taste instead of describing it.

2. Show it, then react

Drop screenshots of designs you like into your Obsidian vault, and it tells you in plain words what you keep choosing. Then you see three very different versions of your real landing page, side by side, and say what you like and don't.

3. Sliders, not prompts

On the one you pick, a panel of about twelve sliders and toggles: colours, fonts, text size, corners, spacing, buttons, light or dark. Where your own photos run out, it can make scenes and backgrounds with Kie.ai, never a real person, and only after you see the cost.

4. Locked, so everything matches

Your colours, fonts and sizes are saved as your Design System, with a do and don't list in your words. Your pages, slides, ads and videos all read it. A check makes sure text is big and clear, and fits a phone.

Good to knowYour Business File gained a new section, §22 Design System. You don't have to do anything; the tool fills it in. Say These Words now has nine pages; this tool is page 7. Thirty-eight tools now.

Try the new thing first

Try the Website Design Studio

On the connector, or already updated (page 4)? Then:

  1. See a short demo first. Type: Show me how the design studio works. Nothing is changed; it's a tour.
  2. Collect what you like. Type: Set up my taste vault. Then screenshot 5 to 15 pages or details you like, including one business in your own field, and drop them in.
  3. Start for real. Type: Make my website look like me. Bring your page words if you have them.
  4. React out loud. "Love the photo, hate the green" is perfect. Plain words are all it needs.
  5. Play with the sliders, save the looks you like, and pick one. It locks your look.
  6. Before it goes live: Is my page ready to go live?
Yours, by designYour screenshots are for inspiration only: nothing from them ever goes on your page. No made-up numbers, no fake "trusted by" logos, and no AI picture of you or a client.

Already installed?

Get this version

On the Build Room connector? Nothing to do: the Website Design Studio is already there. Start a new chat and say Make my website look like me.

Using the Build Room OS folder? Update it this way. Your Business File and everything you've made stay exactly as they are:

  1. Download BuildRoom_OS_2026-11-13.zip from the Members' area.
  2. Unzip it there. Mac: double-click the file. Windows: right-click it and choose Extract All, then Extract. Don't unzip it over your old folder.
  3. Run the update and point it at your Build Room OS folder (drag the folder into the window to paste its path):
    Mac: bash ~/Downloads/BuildRoom_OS/update.sh "/path/to/your/BuildRoom_OS"
    Windows: powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "$HOME\Downloads\BuildRoom_OS\update.ps1" "C:\path\to\your\BuildRoom_OS"
  4. Read what it says. It replaces only the tools, keeps a backup, and confirms your Business File is unchanged.
Stuck?Bring it to the next Build Room session. An update never touches your Business File.

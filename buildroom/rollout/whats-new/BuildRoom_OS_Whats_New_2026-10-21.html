Since last week

What's new, in plain words

1. The LinkedIn Engine NEW

Get clients from LinkedIn without ads and without anything automated. It rewrites your profile so it works like a sales page, writes three posts a week from your stories, and gives you a short daily list of people worth talking to. You do the posting and messaging yourself, about 20 minutes a day.

2. The Event Launcher NEW

Run a live event that sells: a 3-day bootcamp, a 5-day challenge, a workshop or a webinar. It works out how many people you need to invite, writes your emails one at a time, builds the calendar reminders, plans each day, and helps you close. Before anything goes out, it walks you through a checklist so your payment link and follow-up actually work.

3. Everything starts more easily

All 30 tools now respond more reliably when you say their starting words. If one doesn't start, say Where do I start? and it will point you to the right one.

4. Your printed guide grew

Say These Words (the PDF in your Build Room OS folder) now has five pages, including a page for each new tool. Print the new pages and keep them by your computer.

Good to knowYour Business File, the one document that remembers your business, gained a new section for your launches and events. You don't have to do anything; the tools fill it in for you.

Try the new things first

Try the LinkedIn Engine

Already have Build Room OS? First get this week's version (the steps for that are on page 5). Then:

  1. Open Claude in your Build Room OS folder, the way you normally do.
  2. See a short demo first. Type: Show me how the LinkedIn engine works and press Enter. Nothing is changed; it's just a tour.
  3. Start for real. Type: Build my LinkedIn engine
  4. Answer one question at a time. It may ask you to paste your current LinkedIn headline and "About" section. Have LinkedIn open in your web browser.
  5. Copy each piece into LinkedIn yourself. It never logs in to LinkedIn and never posts for you. That keeps your account safe.
  6. Each morning, ask: Who should I message on LinkedIn today?
  7. Once a week, ask: Plan my LinkedIn week
TipYour stories are what make the posts good. When it asks about a client or a lesson, tell it out loud with your phone's voice typing, or type a few honest sentences. It never makes up stories or results.

Try the new things first

Try the Event Launcher

Best once you know what you'd sell at the end of the event (your offer). If you don't have one yet, say Help me build my signature offer first.

  1. Open Claude in your Build Room OS folder.
  2. See a short demo first. Type: Show me how the event launcher works
  3. Start planning. Type: Plan my launch. It asks your goal, your list size and your dates.
  4. Check the numbers. It tells you honestly whether your list can reach your goal.
  5. Approve each email one at a time. Change anything that doesn't sound like you.
  6. Before you invite anyone, ask: Is my launch ready? A short checklist, one item at a time: does the payment link work on your phone, are the reminders on and timed right.
  7. After your event, ask: How did my launch do? You'll get the three biggest lessons for next time.
Plan aheadGive yourself at least 3 to 4 weeks from "Plan my launch" to your event day, and 8 weeks or more if partners will promote it for you.

Already installed?

Get this week's version

You'll download the new folder, then move your Business File into it so nothing you've built is lost.

  1. Download BuildRoom_OS_2026-10-17.zip from the Members' area. It goes into your Downloads folder.
  2. Unzip it. Mac: double-click the file. Windows: right-click it and choose Extract All, then Extract.
  3. Find your old Build Room OS folder (the one you've been using).
  4. Copy your Business File out of the old folder. It's called BUILDROOM_BUSINESS_FILE.md. Right-click it and choose Copy.
  5. Open the new folder and choose Paste. When it asks, choose Replace. (The new folder comes with a blank one; yours replaces it.)
  6. From now on, use the new folder. Keep the old one for a week, just in case, then you can delete it.
Not sure which folder is yours?Open your old Business File in a text app. If you see your business name and your answers, that's the one to copy.

New to Build Room OS?

If you haven't installed it yet

What you need (once)

  1. A computer: Mac or Windows. A phone or tablet won't work for this part.
  2. A paid Claude plan (Pro or Max) from claude.ai. This is what does the thinking.
  3. Claude Code on your computer. If you don't have it yet, open the setup page at code.claude.com/docs/en/setup and follow the steps for your computer, or ask in the Members' area; we'll walk you through it on a call.
  4. The Build Room OS file: BuildRoom_OS_2026-10-17.zip from the Members' area.

Put the folder somewhere safe

  1. Unzip the download. Mac: double-click it. Windows: right-click, Extract All, then Extract.
  2. Move the folder called BuildRoom_OS into your Documents folder. This is your Build Room home; your Business File lives here.
Keep it off cloud-sync foldersUse Documents on your own computer, not OneDrive or iCloud Drive "optimised" storage, so Claude can always read your files.

New to Build Room OS?

Open it and say your first words

On a Mac

  1. Press ⌘ Command + Space, type Terminal, press Enter.
  2. Type cd and one space. Don't press Enter yet.
  3. Drag the BuildRoom_OS folder from Documents into the Terminal window. Press Enter.
  4. Type claude and press Enter.

On Windows

  1. Open the BuildRoom_OS folder in Documents.
  2. Click once in the address bar at the top (where the folder path shows).
  3. Type powershell and press Enter. A blue or black window opens.
  4. Type claude and press Enter.

Your first words

When Claude is ready, type this and press Enter:

I'm new to the Build Room. Set me up.

It asks one question at a time. No wrong answers; stop and come back any time.

Three habits 1. Always open Claude inside your BuildRoom_OS folder.
2. Unsure? Say Where do I start?
3. Never paste passwords or account keys into the chat.

Since last time

What's new, in plain words

1. The Speaking Engine NEW

It gets you in front of rooms full of your buyers with one talk worth booking. It drafts your speaking profile from your Business File and shows you one summary to correct. Clients come first; a fee comes second.

2. Real places, and pitches you send yourself

Every group, event or podcast is listed with the page it was found on and checked against ten warning signs. Each pitch is checked for numbers and claims you never gave. Two follow-ups, then it stops.

3. The Company Blueprint NEW

It writes your business down as roles, results and a few honest numbers: the kinds of work it needs, who is responsible for each, and a one-page agreement for every role.

4. Your numbers, and which job to hand off first

Each role gets one to five numbers. It never makes one up. Then it ranks your own jobs by your own hours and tells you which to hand off first.

Good to knowYour Business File gained §24 Speaking and §25 Company Blueprint. Say These Words now has twelve pages; these tools are pages 9 and 10. Positioning now also records how many promises like yours your buyer has already heard, so your copy and ads lead with the right thing. Forty-one tools now.

Try the new thing first

Try the two new tools

On the connector, or already updated (page 4)? Then:

  1. Start. Type: Get me speaking gigs. Read the summary it shows you and say what's wrong.
  2. Settle one talk. Type: Help me pick a talk topic. One talk a host can book beats five ideas.
  3. Make the page hosts ask for. Type: Make my speaker one-sheet.
  4. Find rooms. Type: Find me places to speak. Start close to home.
  5. Ask. Type: Write my speaker pitch. You send it from your own email.
  6. Each morning: Who should I pitch today? Ten minutes is enough.
And for the Company BlueprintType Build my company blueprint. When it asks how many hours a job takes you, look at last week's calendar. Later: What should I hand off first? and, each week, Review my numbers.

Already installed?

Get this version

On the Build Room connector? Nothing to do: both new tools are already there. Start a new chat and say Get me speaking gigs.

Using the Build Room OS folder? Update it this way. Your Business File and everything you've made stay exactly as they are:

  1. Download BuildRoom_OS_2026-11-15.zip from the Members' area.
  2. Unzip it there. Mac: double-click the file. Windows: right-click it and choose Extract All, then Extract. Don't unzip it over your old folder.
  3. Run the update and point it at your Build Room OS folder (drag the folder into the window to paste its path):
    Mac: bash ~/Downloads/BuildRoom_OS/update.sh "/path/to/your/BuildRoom_OS"
    Windows: powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "$HOME\Downloads\BuildRoom_OS\update.ps1" "C:\path\to\your\BuildRoom_OS"
  4. Read what it says. It replaces only the tools, keeps a backup, and confirms your Business File is unchanged.
Stuck?Bring it to the next Build Room session. An update never touches your Business File.

Since last time

What's new, in plain words

1. The AI Momentum Labs Book Builder NEW

It writes your own non-fiction book: your stories, your method, your way of talking. It asks fifteen questions (most are already in your Business File), then shows you a plan for every chapter. Nothing is written until you approve it.

2. Any size you want

Twelve chapters and about 62,000 words is the starting point. Say Make it ten chapters or ask for a shorter book and it changes. It writes one chapter at a time, and every length is counted.

3. Edited and checked like a real book

An editor's memo, a sample chapter you sign off on, then every section edited. A separate reviewer reads it cold. Every number, quotation and client story is traced back to you, or it comes out.

4. Test readers before it's done

Made-up practice readers find the weak spots. Then six to eight real people read it and answer a short form. A final check says GO or lists what to FIX.

Good to knowYou get a finished manuscript as a Word file. The cover, layout and publishing come after. Your Business File gained §23 Book; the tool fills it in. Say These Words now has ten pages; this tool is page 8. Thirty-nine tools now.

Try the new thing first

Try the Book Builder

On the connector, or already updated (page 4)? Then:

  1. See a short demo first. Type: Show me how the book builder works. It's a tour on a made-up author; nothing of yours is changed.
  2. Plan it. Type: Plan my book. Answer the questions in your own words. Long answers are good.
  3. Gather your material. Talks, posts, podcast transcripts, notes. Say Here's a transcript to use.
  4. Write. Each time you sit down, type: Write the next chapter.
  5. Edit and test. Edit my book, then Have readers test my book.
  6. Before you call it done: Is my book ready?
A book takes many sittingsIt remembers where you stopped. Ask How is my book coming along? any time. It never makes up a fact, a client or a quotation; if it needs more from you, it asks.

Already installed?

Get this version

On the Build Room connector? Nothing to do: the Book Builder is already there. Start a new chat and say Plan my book.

Using the Build Room OS folder? Update it this way. Your Business File and everything you've made stay exactly as they are:

  1. Download BuildRoom_OS_2026-11-14.zip from the Members' area.
  2. Unzip it there. Mac: double-click the file. Windows: right-click it and choose Extract All, then Extract. Don't unzip it over your old folder.
  3. Run the update and point it at your Build Room OS folder (drag the folder into the window to paste its path):
    Mac: bash ~/Downloads/BuildRoom_OS/update.sh "/path/to/your/BuildRoom_OS"
    Windows: powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "$HOME\Downloads\BuildRoom_OS\update.ps1" "C:\path\to\your\BuildRoom_OS"
  4. Read what it says. It replaces only the tools, keeps a backup, and confirms your Business File is unchanged.
Stuck?Bring it to the next Build Room session. An update never touches your Business File.

The Build Room OS

One file. Every session builds on the last. Set up once, in about ten minutes.

★The Big Idea

Every Build Room session now reads and updates one document: your Business File. It carries your avatar, your offer, your positioning, your funnel — everything you've built.

That means you never repeat yourself. Your first session interviews you. Your fourth session already knows your business and just builds. The more sessions you run, the shorter they get and the sharper the output gets.

Three rules, that's it:

  • Bring the file to every session. Paste or attach it when asked. Claude reads it and skips every question it already answers.
  • Save the updated copy at the end. Every session hands the whole file back with a "what changed" summary. Replace your old copy. Done.
  • It's yours. Edit anything by hand, anytime. Your edits always win.

1Install the Skills

In Claude Code or Codex:

  1. Unzip BuildRoom_OS.zip. Keep the folder; your Business File lives in it.
  2. Open a terminal in the folder and run claude or codex.
  3. That's the whole install. The skills load on their own and trigger when you say the words.

2Start With the Navigator

In that terminal, say:

I'm new to the Build Room — set me up

The navigator welcomes you, creates your Business File with a five-minute snapshot of your business, and tells you exactly which session to run first. Any time you're unsure what to do next, just ask: What should I work on next?

Prefer to see it? Open tools/Business_File_Viewer.html from the folder, paste your file, and it shows every section's status, your progress, and the one session to run next — no session needed.

4Where to Keep Your File

  • Save BUILDROOM_BUSINESS_FILE.md (or the copy your first session creates) somewhere you won't lose it — Documents, Notion, Google Drive, anywhere.
  • It's a plain text file. Open it with any notes app. Read it — it's the clearest picture of your business you'll own.
  • One file per business. Running two businesses? Two files.

# Funnel Scorecard + Leak Finder
*"Where is my funnel leaking?"*

The founder's recurring funnel test, as a session. "Every four hours it does a conversion check of every stage of the pipeline and suggests optimizations where the funnel is leaking. And I go, yep, those are great suggestions, do it."

- **A stage table** over every failure point in your funnel map: entered, converted, rate, change against last week, and where the number came from. Nothing is estimated. A stage it can't read is marked UNREAD.
- **If nothing is measured yet,** the first deliverable is the exact pipeline stages to create. Most businesses can't show the failure rate at every step. Installing the measurement is the win.
- **What moved:** leads moved forward, revenue recorded, and the lead follow-up check: has every lead been touched?
- **The top three leaks,** ranked by revenue at risk, with two or three fixes each, routed to the campaign or session that owns the fix.
- **You approve each fix,** or skip it, or give feedback. The skill applies nothing itself.
- **The weekly routine,** shown in full and created only when you say yes. It re-runs the scorecard, proposes, never applies, and fails loud if it can't read.

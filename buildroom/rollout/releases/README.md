# Build Room OS releases

Dated copies of the member zip, so members can download by link. Each is the output of `build-universal-bundle.sh` at the commit it was added. Keep only the current one plus the previous one.

| File | Built | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| `BuildRoom_OS_2026-11-15.zip` | 2026-11-15 | Forty-one skills. **New:** the Speaking Engine ("Get me speaking gigs"): booked to speak where the member's buyers already gather, clients first and fees second. A seventeen-question speaking profile drafted from the Business File and confirmed by the member in one summary; one to three talks a host can book; a bio and a one-page speaker sheet whose quotes come only from the proof bank; real stages recorded only with the page they were found on and the date; a vetting score and ten red flags (pay-to-speak first); a pitch and two follow-ups, each refused if it carries a figure or claim the member never gave; bookings, a debrief after every talk, a dated plan, and a scorecard from the member's own counts that will not judge a rate from fewer than ten pitches. The full talk is handed to the Webinar Builder. **New:** the Company Blueprint ("Build my company blueprint"): the business written down as seats, results and numbers. Three aim questions drafted from the Business File; the functions and who covers each; an accountability chart; a position contract per seat with three to seven checkable results and authority on both sides (vague lines refused); one to five measures per position with exact definitions, the member's own baselines and no benchmarks; a hand-off ranking from the member's own hours; a five-step read of owner dependence; and a review that looks for the cause before the person. A position contract is not an employment contract. Business File 1.11 adds §24 Speaking and §25 Company Blueprint. §4 Positioning gains two fields, market sophistication and the belief to move, which the Copy Engine and Ad Studio read to choose what the first line leads with. Say These Words has twelve pages. |
| `BuildRoom_OS_2026-11-14.zip` | 2026-11-14 | Thirty-nine skills. **New:** the AI Momentum Labs Book Builder ("Write my book"): the member's own non-fiction book in their voice, to a tested manuscript. Fifteen intake questions, most answered from the Business File, with a sufficiency gate; an outline the member approves; chapters drafted one at a time from their answers and a source packet, with a book record so each story is told once and every length is measured; a developmental memo and style guide; a sample-chapter edit the member signs off; a line edit of every section; an independent review and a fact, source and permission audit; simulated readers and six to eight real readers, reported apart, with thresholds; an issue register (P0 to P3) where a fix counts only once verified against a frozen edition; an independent copyedit and regression check; and a gate that says GO or FIX (no dashes, no placeholders, no unsourced numbers or quotations, no invented clients, no open P0 or P1, text unchanged since the edition). Twelve chapters and about 62,000 words by default; chapter count, length and any one chapter's length are the member's to set. Manuscript only: a .docx export; typesetting, cover and publishing are outside the skill. Business File spec 1.9 adds §23 Book. The standing rule that partner course material is never used is removed from the Webinar Builder and the Event Launcher. The printable guide gains page 8 (ten pages). The Book Builder can read the member's Obsidian vault for sources. **Also:** an audit of all 39 tools, the member documents and the installers, with fixes throughout: the Source Watcher's broken YouTube and arXiv calls and its podcast code; no invented proof in the copy, sales, lead and offer sessions; unreviewed ads and videos can no longer ship, and a changed file needs re-approval; the video kit has its own folder; the Build Room folder is the Obsidian vault (the Business File never moves); the update script can no longer be run backwards. |

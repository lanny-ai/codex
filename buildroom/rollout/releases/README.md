# Build Room OS releases

Dated copies of the member zip, so members can download by link. Each is the output of `build-universal-bundle.sh` at the commit it was added. Keep only the current one plus the previous one.

| File | Built | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| `BuildRoom_OS_2026-11-14.zip` | 2026-11-14 | Thirty-nine skills. **New:** the AI Momentum Labs Book Builder ("Write my book"): the member's own non-fiction book in their voice, to a tested manuscript. Fifteen intake questions, most answered from the Business File, with a sufficiency gate; an outline the member approves; chapters drafted one at a time from their answers and a source packet, with a book record so each story is told once and every length is measured; a developmental memo and style guide; a sample-chapter edit the member signs off; a line edit of every section; an independent review and a fact, source and permission audit; simulated readers and six to eight real readers, reported apart, with thresholds; an issue register (P0 to P3) where a fix counts only once verified against a frozen edition; an independent copyedit and regression check; and a gate that says GO or FIX (no dashes, no placeholders, no unsourced numbers or quotations, no invented clients, no open P0 or P1, text unchanged since the edition). Thirteen chapters and about 62,000 words by default; chapter count, length and any one chapter's length are the member's to set. Manuscript only: a .docx export; typesetting, cover and publishing are outside the skill. Business File spec 1.9 adds §23 Book. The standing rule that partner course material is never used is removed from the Webinar Builder and the Event Launcher. The printable guide gains page 8 (ten pages). |
| `BuildRoom_OS_2026-11-13.zip` | 2026-11-13 | Thirty-eight skills. **New:** the Website Design Studio ("Make my website look like me"): a look that is the member's own instead of the generic 2026 AI look. A taste vault of screenshots in the member's Obsidian vault, filed and read back in plain words; three very different directions of the real landing page side by side, at most three rounds; a tweak bar of about twelve sliders and toggles (more behind "advanced") on a page token contract; Kie.ai images through the shared media ledger, never a real person; a lock into a Design System (`tokens.json`, `tokens.css`, `slides.json`, `design-system.md`, `fonts.md`); and a go-live check (18px text for a 50+ audience, 4.5:1 contrast, phone fit, no taste-vault images, image rights, numbers only from the Business File, eight generic tells named). Business File spec 1.8 adds §22 Design System; Lead Capture, Offer Page Copy, the Webinar Builder, the Ad Studio and the Video Director read it before designing. The printable guide gains page 7 (nine pages). |

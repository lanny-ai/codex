# Build Room OS releases

Dated copies of the member zip, so members can download by link. Each is the output of `build-universal-bundle.sh` at the commit it was added. Keep only the current one plus the previous one.

| File | Built | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| `BuildRoom_OS_2026-11-21.zip` | 2026-11-21 | Forty-two skills. **New:** your first session now starts by asking for your website: if you have one and your Claude can browse the web, it reads your page and shows you what it learned about your business, for you to correct, before asking only what is missing. You finish with your business on one designed page, in your own words, plus three drafted ways to say what you do: one sentence, one paragraph and a short bio. The guide now shows a finished example for a made-up business, so you can see what you will get before you start. Also new: a LinkedIn Prospector inside the LinkedIn Engine ("Find me leads on LinkedIn"): it turns your ideal client into the exact searches to run, starting with people you are already connected to; you bring back up to twenty people you looked at, each with one real thing you noticed; it writes one opening line per person from that and nothing else, never a pitch; and when someone replies it gives you the next question to ask, one at a time, until a call is earned. Each day it builds one page with a card per person: a button that opens their profile, the words to copy, and a tick when it is done. A stranger gets a drafted comment before a connection request. It knows what to do with the replies that are not a neat answer, such as "what do you charge?", "send me some info" and "not right now", and a "no" is final. Before a booked call it writes a one-page brief in the person's own words. On posting days the same page starts with today's post ("Write today's LinkedIn post"): it finds a timely hook with its source, writes the post from your own story or view, and makes a branded image for it, with the words to copy, the picture to save and the alt text. You choose three, five or seven posts a week; three is the default. An AI-made picture is optional, at your own cost, and you publish every post yourself. You run every search and send every message yourself, so your account stays safe, and nothing here promises replies or clients. Also: a short interview for anyone with little written down ("Interview me about my business"): easy questions first, about ten minutes, any of them skippable, and you get your business on one page in your own words. Autopilot ("Run autopilot") now works best after that or after "Research my business first". **Upgraded:** Autopilot now checks every draft before it hands them over, and will not finish while a draft holds a line you retired, a number nobody confirmed, a guarantee you do not offer, or a client's words you have not been given permission to use. It asks once, after your go-ahead, whether to leave out steps that would come back mostly blank, such as an opt-in page when you have no free resource. The Your Build page says plainly when a run started on too little, and shows the six sessions most members reach for next with the rest one click away. "Research my business first" asks the easy questions before the hard ones, never asks again about something you answered with "none", sets old material apart, and stops after one extra round of questions. **Also:** your Business File has four new lines in section 1: how someone books, your background, a typical client result, and what you refuse to claim, which every session now respects. On request, Autopilot can prepare the wording from an approved run as a values file for a funnel template ("Prepare my GoHighLevel values"); it prepares a file only and connects to nothing. The Search tool no longer treats your own price as a results claim. Say These Words now has thirteen pages, with Autopilot on page 2. Everything Autopilot makes is still a draft: nothing is published, sent or paid for, and nothing here promises results. |
| `BuildRoom_OS_2026-11-19.zip` | 2026-11-19 | Forty-two skills. **New:** Autopilot ("Run autopilot"): drafts your ideal client, offer and positioning without interviewing you, shows them to you on one review page where every line is tagged as yours, from your files, or drafted for you, and waits for your go-ahead. Then it drafts your offer page, funnel, opt-in page, sales script, follow-up and a lead gen plan straight through, and ends with a Your Build page: a link to each draft and a checklist of what still needs you. It works best after "Research my business first"; on a thin file it tells you so and asks before going on. Everything is a draft; nothing is published, sent or paid for. It needs Cowork, Claude Code or Codex, and it saves its place so you can carry on later. If you have already built your first modules, it keeps what you built and fills the gaps. **Upgraded:** the Discovery Engine now runs Deep Discovery on your own business ("Research my business first"): it shows you a list of file names and reads only what you agree to, researches your files, your public pages and your market, shows you a "What I found" page with a source for every fact, sends anything your sources disagree on back to you, asks at most ten questions, and tells you how ready your Business File is. It also collects your clients' real words, real results and samples of your own voice for later sessions. **New pages:** the Quick Start Guide at guide.aimomentumlabs.com lists every tool with the words that start it, and guide.aimomentumlabs.com/start walks a new member through connecting step by step. **Fixed:** opening your personal link in a browser now shows how to add it to Claude instead of an error, and the join page asks before replacing a link you already have. |

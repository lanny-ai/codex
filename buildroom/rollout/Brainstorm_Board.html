Brainstorm Board

Frame one question. Get every idea out fast. Cluster, vote, decide — then copy the whole thing into Claude and say Capture what we decided. Nothing leaves this page until you copy it.

1 · Frame it

A brainstorm with no question produces a list. Write the one question this session answers.

2 · Diverge

Quantity. No judging, no discussing. Type an idea, press Enter, keep going. Change who's talking with the dropdown.

10:00Stop when it hits zero — the last two minutes are the good ones.
0 ideas

3 · Cluster

Drag ideas into groups (or tap an idea, then tap a group). Name each group by the job it does, not the feature. Three to six groups is normal.

4 · Dot-vote

Everyone gets the same number of dots. Pick your name, tap ideas. You can stack dots on one idea. Vote alone first, then talk.

5 · Decide

Top ideas by votes. For each: what kind of thing is it, who owns it, and the first step. "We" is not an owner. If it isn't decided, leave the type blank and add the question below.

Anything with votes can be decided; anything without goes to the log as floated.

Open questions

What got raised and not answered. These open the next brainstorm.

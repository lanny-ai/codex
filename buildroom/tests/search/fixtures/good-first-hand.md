# How much does a bookkeeping clean-up cost for a small business?

**Short answer:** For a business with one bank account and under a year of mess, a clean-up usually costs between $900 and $2,400 with us, and takes two to four weeks [own]. The price doubles when there is a second entity or payroll that was never reconciled [own]. Most clients land near the middle of that range [own].

By Dana Ruiz, CPA since 1998. Updated: 2026-11-03

## What decides the price?

Three things: how many months are behind, how many accounts feed the books, and whether payroll was run outside the system [method]. A single account with six months behind is the simplest job we see [own]. Our clean-ups took a median of 11 working hours in 2025 [own]. See the [weekly cash check](/answers/weekly-cash-check) once the books are clean.

## What doubles it?

- A second company sharing the same bank account
- Payroll run by hand with no filings matched
- Receipts kept nowhere

Call (555) 010-0100 if you are not sure which you have, or allow a 20-minute call.

## How we know

We counted every clean-up we did in 2025: 38 jobs, from 3 months behind to 26 months behind, for owners with one to three entities [own]. We did not count jobs we quoted and did not win. The hours are from our time records, not estimates [own].

## What we'd check first

Before any price, we ask for the last bank statement and the last tax return. If they do not agree with each other, that gap is the job, and we price the gap, not the months. Most clients who were quoted a flat monthly fee elsewhere were being charged for the gap without knowing it. That one question has saved more people money than anything else on this page.

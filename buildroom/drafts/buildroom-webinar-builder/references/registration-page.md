# The registration page words

The page build belongs to **Lead Capture** or **Offer Page Copy**; the confirmation email, reminders, calendar file and follow-up belong to the **Event Launcher**. This skill writes the words that come from the talk, so the page promises exactly what the hour delivers.

## 1. The promise and the title

- **The promise**: what they will be able to do or decide after the hour, in one sentence a 70-year-old would repeat to a friend. Concrete, not a money promise ("make $10,000") unless a typical buyer achieves it and the proof bank shows it.
- **Three title options** through Kern: an outcome title ("How to … without …"), a question title, and a "the [number] [things] that …" title matching the three teaching blocks. The member picks.

## 2. The page (`webinar/registration-page.md`)

In this order:
1. The headline (the chosen title) and a one-line subhead with the promise.
2. Who it is for, in the avatar's words (§2), and who it is not for.
3. **Three bullets**, one per teaching block: what they will learn, phrased as the true belief or the first step.
4. **When**: the date, the start time with a time-zone table, and how long it runs ("60 minutes, finishing on time").
5. **Who presents, live**: the member's name, photo, a two-line bio with an honest detail. Name any guest who will actually appear, and only them.
6. **The reassurance block** (`older-audience.md`): live (or "recorded", said plainly); there will be an offer at the end, and nobody has to buy; nobody will call unless they ask; it is recorded and the replay is available until a stated date; join from a computer, tablet or phone.
7. The registration form: name and email only, unless a question genuinely shapes the talk (one at most: "What's the one thing you'd like answered?").
8. The privacy line and the recording notice.

Proof on the page follows the proof bank and `honesty-rules.md`: an ID, a class, the typical result beside any number.

## 3. What goes to the Event Launcher

The title, the promise, the three bullets, the date and time, the replay expiry and the reassurance block, so its confirmation and reminders say the same things. Note the hand-off in the launch folder's `plan.md`.

Opening

You can probably tell me your production to the dollar.

Can you tell me your overhead?

Most owners I meet guess 55 to 60 percent. It is usually higher. And when you do not know that number, you end up judging the month by the bank balance.

The problem

The books in most practices get done at night, by a spouse or by the office manager, on top of everything else. Nobody chose that. It just ended up that way.

So the numbers arrive late, or once a year, from your CPA. By then there is nothing to do about them except feel bad.

Meanwhile the questions that matter stay open. Can the practice afford another hire? Is the lab bill creeping up? What do you actually take home?

You can't fix what nobody is looking at.

What changes

The Clean Books Program takes the monthly bookkeeping off your practice completely.

Your books are closed by the 10th of every month. You get one page, the Practice Scorecard, and a 30-minute call with me to go through it. Collections. Overhead percentage. Hygiene production. Owner pay.

I spent nine years as the office manager of a three-chair dental practice before I started Northline in 2021. I kept the books there because nobody else would. I know what a lab fee is and where it belongs.

What you get

  • Books closed by the 10th of each month
  • A one-page Practice Scorecard: collections, overhead percentage, hygiene production, owner pay
  • A monthly 30-minute numbers call with me
  • Payroll and lab-fee coding cleaned up
  • A year-end package handed to your CPA

How it runs

  1. Weeks 1 to 3: cleanup. We fix the last 12 months and connect the practice software.
  2. Month 2 onward: the monthly close. Books closed by the 10th, your scorecard, your call.
  3. Each quarter: an overhead review with a target.

What I do not do

I do not do taxes, and nothing here is tax advice. I work beside your CPA and make sure your tax person never has to guess.

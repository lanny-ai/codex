Brand new? Four steps and one sentence.

Connect Build Room OS to Claude or Codex once. Then say one sentence, and it starts building the file that remembers your business. Plan on about fifteen minutes.

What you need before you start

  • A Build Room membership. You will use the email you joined with, and the room code your host gives you.
  • Claude or Codex. Claude needs a paid plan to add a connector. The Claude desktop app is the easiest place to begin.
1

Get your personal link

  1. Open the join page. It opens in a new tab so you can keep these steps in view.
  2. Type your name and the email you use for the Build Room.
  3. Type the room code from your host. If you paste it, check there is no blank space before or after it.
  4. Press Get my link.
What you should see: your personal link, farther down on the same page, with a Copy button. It is not sent by email. If the page says the team will check your membership, come back a little later and enter the same email.
Keep it to yourself. The link is yours alone. It goes into Claude or Codex, not into your web browser.
2

Add it to Claude or Codex

Pick the one you use. You only do this once.

For the Claude desktop app and claude.ai.

  1. Open Claude. Choose Customize, then Connectors. On some versions it is Settings, then Connectors.
  2. Choose Add custom connector. You may find it behind a plus or Add button.
  3. In the name box, type Build Room OS.
  4. In the second box, paste your personal link.
  5. If Claude asks how to sign in, choose No sign in. Your link already proves who you are.
  6. Press Add. If it then shows a Connect button, press that too.
  7. Claude lists what the connector may do. Allow the items so sessions can run without stopping to ask.
What you should see: Build Room OS in your list of connectors, with a check mark beside it.

For Claude Code in a terminal.

  1. On the join page, under your link, there is a line that begins claude mcp add. It already has your key in it. Copy that line.
  2. Paste it into your terminal and press Enter. It has this shape:
  1. Start Claude Code in the folder for your business, then type /mcp to check.
What you should see: buildroom in the list, shown as connected.

For Codex in a terminal, the Codex IDE extension, or the ChatGPT desktop app.

In a terminal

  1. Run this line, with your own personal link in place of the capital letters. Keep the quotation marks.
codex mcp add buildroom --url "YOUR-PERSONAL-LINK"
  1. Run codex mcp list, or start Codex and type /mcp, to check.

In the IDE extension or the desktop app

  1. Open the gear menu or Settings, then MCP servers, then Add server.
  2. Name it buildroom, choose Streamable HTTP, and paste your personal link as the URL.
  3. Save, then restart the extension or the app.
What you should see: buildroom in the list of MCP servers, turned on. It needs no sign in.
3

Check it works

  1. Start a new chat.
  2. In the Claude app, open the tools menu under the message box and turn Build Room OS on.
  3. Type this:
What Build Room sessions do I have?
What you should see: a list of Build Room sessions. That means you are connected.
4

Say the first sentence

This is the quick start. Type it exactly, or in your own words.

I'm new here. Help me get started.

What happens next

  1. A short welcome. A few sentences on how the Build Room works.
  2. Six quick questions about your business, one at a time. Plan on about five minutes. Short, honest answers are fine.
  3. Your Business File is created. It is one document that every later session reads and adds to, so you never explain your business twice.
  4. One next step. It recommends the Ideal Client Avatar session first, and gives you the words to start it.
Already have a filled-in Business File? Skip this sentence. Attach your file to the chat, or open Claude or Codex in the folder that holds it, and ask What should I work on next?
5

Keep your Business File

The file is called BUILDROOM_BUSINESS_FILE.md. It lives on your computer. Build Room OS never stores it, so keeping it safe is the one job that is yours.

If you use a regular Claude chat

  1. At the end of a session Claude gives you the updated file. Save it to a folder you will remember, over the older copy.
  2. Next time, start a new chat, turn Build Room OS on, and attach the file with the plus or paperclip button before you ask for anything.

If you use Cowork, Claude Code or Codex

  1. Make one folder for your business and always open the app in that folder.
  2. The file is read from that folder and saved back there. You do not attach anything.
What you should see: in later sessions Claude mentions things from your file, such as your ideal client or your offer, without asking for them again.

Already done your first modules? Run Autopilot

If you have built your ideal client, your offer and your positioning, you do not need to start over. Autopilot keeps what you built, shows it to you on one page to check, then drafts the rest: your offer page, funnel, opt-in page, sales script, follow-up and a lead gen plan.

  1. Open Claude in the folder that holds your Business File, with Build Room OS turned on. Use Cowork, Claude Code or Codex. Autopilot does not run in a regular chat, because it needs a folder to save its work.
  2. Optional, about 15 minutes. If you have testimonials, client emails, call transcripts or your own posts saved somewhere, say this first. It shows you a list of file names and reads only what you agree to. The drafts then use your clients' real words and sound like you.
Research my business first
  1. Then say this:
Run autopilot

What happens next

  1. A few quick questions, only for things your file does not already answer, such as your price, your guarantee and how people book.
  2. One page to review. Your ideal client, offer and positioning, with every line tagged as yours, from your files, or drafted for you. Mark what to change and say go.
  3. The rest is drafted for you. Plan on a couple of hours of it working in the background. It saves its place, so if it stops, say "Run autopilot" again and it carries on.
  4. One page at the end. What was drafted, with a link to each piece, and a checklist of what still needs you.
Good to know: everything it makes is a draft for you to review. Nothing is published, sent or paid for. Sections you already built are kept, and only the gaps are filled. If you want one redone, ask for that.
Not much written down? Skip the research step and say this instead. It asks you a short set of easy questions, about ten minutes, and gives you your business on one page in your own words. Autopilot then has real answers to work from.
Interview me about my business

If something goes wrong

What you seeWhat to do
You clicked your link and saw a page saying it goes into ClaudeYour link is fine. It is not a web page. Copy it and paste it into Claude or Codex, as in step 2.
The join page shows an errorType the room code again by hand, with no space before or after it.
“The Build Room team will check your membership”Wait a little, then go back to the join page and enter the same email.
The join page says you already have a linkYour link still works. Only ask for a new one if you really lost it, because a new link turns the old one off.
Claude asks about signing inChoose No sign in.
The connector is listed but says ConnectPress Connect.
Your screen does not match these stepsCheck whether the app is asking to update. Update it and try again.
Claude says your Business File is blank or it cannot find itAttach your filled-in file, or open the app in the folder that holds it. See step 5.
You used the zip folder before and wonder if you have the newest toolsYou do not need to do anything. The first time you use Build Room OS in that folder, Claude or Codex offers to move the old copies into a backup folder. Say yes. Your Business File and your work are not touched.
Anything elseTake a screenshot and send it to your host.

Quick Start Guide

Start in three steps

About ten minutes, one time. After that you only talk to Claude.

1

Get your personal link

Open the join page. Enter your name, your Build Room email and the room code from your host. Your link appears on the page.

2

Add it to Claude

In Claude, open Customize (or Settings), then Connectors, then Add custom connector. Name it Build Room OS, paste your link, choose No sign in, and connect.

3

Say what you want

Start a new chat, turn Build Room OS on in the tools menu, and type any phrase on this page. Claude finds the right tool and runs it with you.

What Build Room OS will and won’t do

The tools are built to use your own facts. They do not make up numbers, testimonials or credentials, and they do not promise leads, bookings or revenue. Your Business File and everything you make stay on your own computer.

You need Claude on a paid plan. A few tools use your own accounts to finish the job, such as a CRM for funnels or an image and video service for ads.

## 1. What matters most (10 lines)

1. **The event is the accelerant, not the funnel.** Put a free, self-serve "first win" tool (the Navigator, which produces a Compass) in front of the event and get people to finish it before Day 1. About 500 Compasses were done before doors opened, at 92% completion (`K/forever-free-evergreen-audit.md` §1, §6).
2. **Install one belief before you invite.** Three belief emails (opportunity, identity, mechanism), then an invitation that only sells the free seat and live attendance. Never sell the offer in the invitation (`PB/july-2026-bootcamp-belief-framework.md`; `PB/july-2026-bootcamp-invitation-sequence.md` Notes).
3. **Live building, not lecture.** Every attendee produces something on Day 1. "A total stranger, often 70+ and anti-tech, walked in scared and walked out with a plan / website / first sale" (`PB/forever-free-bootcamp-PLANNING-BRIEF.md` §8).
4. **Expect decay and engineer show-up on every day, not just Day 1.** July went 969 registered → 380 → 257 → 133 → 67 at the close. The Day 2 and Day 3 reminders misfired, and no Day-1-night or Day-2-night email existed (audit §0, §1.5).
5. **A tiny first transaction changes the relationship.** The $1 Build Brief (102 buyers) made the $997 a one-click upsell. Lanny's figures: "One-click upsells can convert at… 20, 30, 40, 50%" vs "3-5%" when people must re-enter details (`ZT/...2026-08-05...ffa1a6ff.md` 00:43:32).
6. **Proof must be honest and classified.** Free-camp wins are separated from paid-member and external results (classes A–E). The one integrity breach in July (a paid-program member's $30K presented as a free-camp result) is called out in the audit (`PB/forever-free-bootcamp-PROOF-BANK.md`; audit §1.2).
7. **Test every checkout path on the morning of the close day.** Checkout broke at peak intent at two camps in a row (Feb: order forms and order bump; Jul: Stripe identity collision). In September nobody could buy at all (audit §2 Day 3; `K/events/2026-09-20-liftoff-offer-workshop-prep.md` §0).
8. **Decide deliberately whether the close day has a human close.** July had "zero" structured close and still converted 21 of 102 dollar-buyers. Nobody tested what a 20-minute structured close would have added (audit §2 Day 3).
9. **Follow-up is where the money leaks.** All ten lifecycle workflows sat in draft after the camp, with 2,181 enrollments frozen. 51 people paid $1 and never saw the $997 offer, even though 56% of those who did see the price bought (`K/forever-free-followup-redesign.md` §7).
10. **Write every price, term, date and link down before the event.** Undocumented custom terms, missing replay links and inferred cohort dates kept costing money and trust (`K/sales/meet-them-on-terms-playbook.md`; `FB/patterns.md`; `FB/knowledge-gaps.md`).

## 2. Lanny's method as a timeline

### 2.1 DECIDE: offer, the one belief, event format

**Format.** The format is a free, live, multi-day Zoom event with a value-first arc that ends in a closing-day offer. "Day 1 ~3h, Day 2 ~3h, Day 3 'anything goes' (runs long, the close)". Replays run about 7 days (`PB/forever-free-bootcamp-PLANNING-BRIEF.md` §1). The format held across runs, and only the tooling evolved: "The format is stable across runs; the tooling and Day-2/3 polish evolve" (§3). Variants tested:
- 3-day free boot camp (core).
- 5-day challenge with a sprint promise: "The promise of this program was make five… $5, five days, 5,000… What I wanted you to discover is… a gear that you never knew you had" (`IT/transcript session 2 max produ 5 day challenge.md` ~01:11–01:12).
- Paid 3-day intensive with VIP seats (Sep 2026 "Built for Freedom" / "3-Day AI MBA": "$1,295 a seat; two seats ride with every $27 Personal Edition… VIP $497 a seat", `K/products/13-days-to-done/3-day-ai-mba-curriculum.md`).
- Post-sale "live buyer bonus" session (`PB/2026-07-30-build-lab-bonus-session-agenda.md`).

**Pick the format by where the sale happens.** "Webinar funnels (Pattern A) end in a booked call. Live-event funnels (Pattern B) end in butts in seats for a stage close. Pick the pattern by where the actual sale happens" (`K/sales/event-and-webinar-funnel-architectures.md`).

**The one belief (Lanny calls it the "big domino", a term he borrows from Brunson).** Write it as one sentence. July's version: "AI is the single biggest wealth leverage of my lifetime, the window to grab it is open right now, and it works for someone like me…" (`PB/july-2026-bootcamp-belief-framework.md`). Under it sit three supporting beliefs, each replacing one false belief (opportunity / identity / mechanism). Sequence logic: "want it (1), then qualify yourself in (2), then believe it is doable (3)."
- Reframe rule: "Sell the freedom, not the technology… The moment it smells like homework, we lose them."
- Urgency rule: "Do not manufacture fake urgency. The real 'left behind' fear is enough."

**The offer ladder (decide before you promote).**
- Planned July ladder: $987/$997 Engine → ~$2,997 Build Room → $10,000/yr Platinum (`PB/july-2026-bootcamp-master.md`). The strategy page added "Every dollar paid at any level credits toward the next" and a Day-3 "48-Hour Buy Now Special" bundle (`PB/july-2026-bootcamp-strategy-page.html`).
- Actual July ladder: Compass (free) → Blueprint (free) → **$1 Build Brief** → **Build Lab $997 or 12 × $137** → Build Room ~$3,000 → Complete Year $9,000 (audit §0 "offer stack as actually sold").
- Lesson: the ladder changed between plan and event, and `knowledge/products/price-ladder.md` still does not exist (`FB/knowledge-gaps.md`).

**Free-front-end logic.** "Free front-end logic modeled on Vistaprint (give the front end, monetize the ascension)" (planning brief §11). Harv's rule: "The headaches are in the front end. The money is in the back end… You've got to have the back end to support it" (`K/products/13-days-to-done/book-plus-live-bonus-pitch-model.md`). This is Harv's IP; see §5.

**Offer quality checklist (Lanny's own vault distillation).** "One person, one problem… A specific promise, denominated in something they can hold… Bonuses kill objections… A guarantee that carries the risk… Honest urgency and scarcity… Name it like a result" (`K/events/2026-09-20-liftoff-offer-workshop-prep.md` §1). It draws on Hormozi and Sam Ocean; see §5.

### 2.2 BUILD: pages, calendar, reminders, proof

**Assets list (transferable).**
- From the Dolf de Roos funnel: "Lead magnet, indoctrination page + video… Waitlist page, expiring pages, countdown timers… Webinar invite + reminder emails + reminder SMS… Objection-handling and countdown sequences… cart-abandon sequence, one-click upsell… recordings-only offer for non-buyers, payment-plan order form… Customer-experience onboarding" (`K/sales/automated-webinar-email-funnel.md`).
- HEI challenge page inventory (structure only): invite page (list and FB) → TY page with OTO for the live event → separate final TY pages for "YES VIP Pass" and "NO VIP Pass" → daily Facebook Live per day → sales page with bonuses → cart → replay page (`K/hei-master-log/campaigns/hei-challenges.md`).
- HEI webclass inventory: reg / TY / pre-training 1 and 2 / recap / replay / "Replay Non-Attendee" / expired-offer / waitlist / encore reg pages (`K/hei-master-log/programs/ffc.md`; `K/hei-master-log/infrastructure/url-creation-template.md`, which also gives a URL naming convention with "/green" for evergreen variants).

**Personalized first-win tool.** In July the Navigator (22 questions) produced a Compass. The Architect then produced a Blueprint, and $1 bought a Build Brief. "Each phase was designed to deliver standalone value, not just advance towards a sale" (Lanny, `ZT/...2026-08-05...ffa1a6ff.md` 00:43:32).
- Why first-party matters: Lanny moved the tools from custom GPTs to his own web app because "everybody didn't have paid ChatGPT" (an attendee, quoted in audit §2 Day 2). The move also gave him data on every user, where "with custom GPTs… the only thing you can see is how many people, approximately" (Lanny 00:11:47).
- Identity capture: the promo link embedded the email in the URL "so… I had a record of every single person's interaction from the very beginning". This later caused data loss when people forwarded links (audit §2 Day 1c). See §4.

**Greased slide in every form.** "The first question has to be slippery… Resistance is highest when a process first starts" (`ZT/2026-07-28-2100-...d340d50e.md` 00:50:10). Data check: 30% of starters quit on question 2, a heavy values question placed right after the easy warmup (`K/forever-free-followup-redesign.md` §3). The same rule applies to challenge opt-ins: "If you ask for first name, last name, email, choose country, mobile phone, a checkbox… nobody's gonna fill that out… why not just put them right into the challenge right there?" (`ZT/2026-09-02-2102-Build-Lab-Bonus-Session-...81891ea7.md` ~00:23).

**Calendar files.**
- One .ics file with one VEVENT per day, each with its own summary ("Forever Free Boot Camp - Day 1/2/3"), the join link, and a pre-work reminder on Day 1 ("Bring your Compass") (`PB/forever-free-bootcamp.ics`).
- AddEvent widget: "+ Add All 3 Days to Calendar", `RRULE FREQ=DAILY;COUNT=3`, timezone `America/Los_Angeles`, 15-minute alarm, a per-contact merge field for the Zoom link (`{{ contact.zoom_event_join_link }}`), and a day-by-day promise in the description (`PB/add-to-calendar-widget.html`).
- Email-safe HTML button block that links to the hosted widget (`PB/add-to-calendar-email-block.html`).
- Timezone discipline: "Stated as '2:00 PM Pacific Standard Time,' but late July is Pacific DAYLIGHT time… Copy says '2:00 PM Pacific'" (`PB/july-2026-bootcamp-master.md`). The confirmation email lists PT/MT/CT/ET/London and a converter link (`PB/phase1-nurture-FINAL-july13-19.md`, Confirmation email).

**Proof bank before copy.** Classify every story: "(A) FREE-CAMP WIN — transformation… (B) FREE-CAMP WIN — cash… (C) FREEDOM / IMPACT… (D) PAID-PROGRAM WIN… NOT usable as free-camp proof… (E) EXTERNALLY-CITED EXAMPLE… (U) UNCERTAIN" (`PB/forever-free-bootcamp-PROOF-BANK.md`). Rules: "Do not dress a paid-member or external number as a free-camp attendee result"; "DON'T OVERSTATE PIPELINE AS CLOSED." The pre-event evidence audit was flagged as a gap and never done before July (`FB/knowledge-gaps.md` "July campaign plans — evidence audit").

**Ops pre-flight (from what broke).** Host the app on cloud infrastructure, not office internet. Dry-run the reminder schedule 24 hours before. Test checkout "with a real card, from a non-US IP, on the morning of Day 3". Rehearse breakout rooms or don't use them (audit §5, "What automation replaces"). The bonus-session host checklist adds: "Name one person to watch chat and one person to capture unresolved blockers… End with the exact next action visible on screen" (`PB/2026-07-30-build-lab-bonus-session-agenda.md`).

### 2.3 FILL: invitation sequence, segments, partners

**Sequence shape (Lanny's default skeleton, used across many funnels).**
- Pattern A, webinar to appointment: "Pre-Frame Story Sequence (3 emails, 1 day apart)… Invitation Sequence (5 emails, 1 day apart)… Reminder Sequence (3 messages)… No-Show Sequence (2 messages)". Branches: registered vs not, attended vs no-show (`K/sales/event-and-webinar-funnel-architectures.md`).
- Pattern B, live event: adds "Multi-channel retargeting… A Video Sales Letter asset… for non-openers… Countdown / Closeout Sequence (3 emails)".
- "Do not lead with the register link. Earn the click with the pre-frame."

**July 2026 as actually shipped (16 emails, all from Harv in first person; `PB/phase1-nurture-FINAL-july13-19.md`).**
- **Phase 1 (Jul 13–19), 7 daily emails at 9:00 AM PT.** "No boot camp mention until Email 7. The only CTA in Emails 1–6 is the free Navigator". Arc: founder story ("He was making great money. And he resented every dollar of it.") → why → "This is not a personality test" (concede the objection, "probably nothing", then reframe to utility) → give away one actual question → personalized page delivered → 15-minute Compass Debrief calls → Email 7, "Something's happening July 27th — wanted you to know first." The audit called this "the single best-performing asset in this entire funnel."
- **Phase 2 (Jul 20–26), 7 daily invitation emails.** Day-by-day → anchor proof story → "You may have seen me promote this before" (repeat-attendee objection) → "Three days is a lot" (time objection plus value math) → "Is this the right event for you?" (disqualification: "Lanny's room is not a classroom. It's a workshop… on Day 2, you are going to try to sell something") → fence-sitters → final word.
- **Event-day emails:** 6:00 AM "Today is the day", then 1:45 PM "15 minutes." with the join link.
- **Confirmation email** sent immediately: join link, three per-day add-to-calendar links, a timezone table, and one pre-work action (finish the Navigator).

**Earlier invitation draft rules (`PB/july-2026-bootcamp-invitation-sequence.md`).**
- "Every CTA is the same: save the free seat, then show up live. One decision, repeated."
- Tease the close-day offer only: "an offer I have never made before."
- "Why free" answered honestly: "at the end, a small number of you are going to want to go further with me. That is how this works."
- "Do not plan to 'just catch the replay'… The replay watchers mean well, and most of them never press play."

**Segments.**
- List temperature tiers (hot/warm/cool/cold × business/consumer). The cold list gets eight value-only emails with engagement scoring: open 1, click 3, reply 6, self-segment reply 8, hand-raise keyword 10 (`PB/july-2026-cold-nurture-campaign.md`).
- Reply prompts at the end of every belief email "self-segment" readers by temperature, objection and demand (`PB/july-2026-bootcamp-content-emails.md`, Engagement notes).
- Deliverability: "Send from a subdomain… Ramp volume… Sunset policy" (cold nurture). The Apr 2026 bonus day showed what happens without it: "Surprise there's only 6 people… did the email… go into spam?" (`K/meetings/forever-free-bootcamp/2026-04-13.md`).
- Speed to contact for the top tier: "iMessage from Lanny within 10 minutes of list entry (Tier A only)" (`PB/july-2026-bootcamp-strategy-page.html`).

**Partners / JV.**
- Lanny's own top lever historically: "getting JV partners to promote my offer and getting 'em to promote it more". He approached them by leading with a give: "I'm looking for offers to promote. I have a mailing list of…" (`IT/transcript session 2 max produ 5 day challenge.md` ~00:37–00:42).
- Partner vetting by EPC: "what's your earnings per click, and do you have screenshots to back it up?" (`ZT/...2026-08-05...ffa1a6ff.md` 02:13:41).
- Partner-sent link mechanics: "Harv Eker is an affiliate… they send emails" (00:11:47).
- Affiliate rules from the UP launch: "Pay affiliates after the event… let them choose cash or product… require the affiliate to be a registered attendee" (`IT/up-event-launch-campaign-decomposition.md`).
- Partner-fronted promotion risk: see §4 (the Harv bait-and-switch moment).

**Decompose a big launch into owned campaigns.** "If three people say they can work on it, it usually never gets done unless somebody takes responsibility." Campaigns: room-block, affiliate, keynote/speaker, internal database, external ads, sponsorship. Run a KPI dashboard per campaign (`IT/up-event-launch-campaign-decomposition.md`).

### 2.4 SHOW-UP ENGINEERING: calendar files, reminders, day-before content

- **Pre-work makes showing up valuable.** "If you've done the Navigator and have your Compass, you arrive with a head start" (Email 8). "Day 1 starts where previous boot camps ended" (Email 10) (`PB/phase1-nurture-FINAL-july13-19.md`).
- **The day before:** a "how to prepare" email. "Show up live… Be at a computer, not just your phone… Come ready to actually do it" (`PB/july-2026-bootcamp-invitation-sequence.md` Email 6).
- **Day-of:** morning email, then a 15-minute email (`PB/phase1-...` Emails 15–16). The Dolf funnel uses a "15 minute" SMS (`K/sales/automated-webinar-email-funnel.md`). Indoctrination emails between registration and the event "pre-sell the idea and the presenter so they show up primed" (same).
- **Missing in July and named as the cheapest win:** "a Day-1-night recap plus Day-2 tease, and a Day-2-night 'tomorrow is the one'" (audit §1.5).
- **Planned show-up hook:** "On Day 1 live, we run your Compass through the Architect… Can't happen from the recording." Target "65%+ of registrants (past average was ~33%)" (`PB/july-2026-bootcamp-strategy-page.html`). Actual was 39.2% (audit §0).
- **What did NOT work: gating content to force live attendance.** "I did the access code that you have to show up live to get it, as a way to get people to show up" (`ZT/2026-07-27-2057-...435c94f4.md` line ~120). He removed it live: "the access code was just deployed to get people to show up anyway, so why don't we just remove that part" (02:35:30). Debrief: "That gate didn't work as well as I hoped… It just created friction" (`ZT/...2026-08-05...` 00:43:32).
- **Lanny's day-over-day rule of thumb:** "Day 1 is always high. Day 2 is always about, you know, 80% or so of day… one, and then… probably another 80% show up on day 3" (`ZT/...2026-08-05...` 00:34:42). Note the conflict: the audit says prior camps ran "roughly 50% Day 1→Day 2" (audit §0). See §3.
- **Reward showing up publicly.** The Day 2 open reads out the "Founding 58" dollar-buyers by name (`ZT/2026-07-28-2100-...` line 26). Day 3: "you're one of the 133, and I want to acknowledge you for that" (`ZT/2026-07-29-2100-...404f47b4.md`).

### 2.5 RUN: day-by-day arc, run-of-show, roles, chat, testimonials on Day 2

**Arc.** "Belief on Day 1. Proof on Day 2. Ascension on Day 3" (`PB/july-2026-bootcamp-strategy-page.html`). The confirmation email's version: "Day 1 is clarity. Day 2 is revenue. Day 3 is momentum. Missing one is like leaving at halftime" (`PB/phase1-...`).
- **Day 1:** belief first ("If you don't believe that it's possible, then it won't happen"), then a voice-driven personal tool and a near-universal "holy crap" moment. Rule: "Move one guaranteed-win build into the first 20 minutes" (audit §5).
- **Day 2:** put it to work with a timed action challenge ("sell a custom app in 17 minutes" produced a dozen live sales in Jul 2025; planning brief §5) and the economics teaching ("acquisition cost must be less than day-one revenue", audit §2 Day 2). 5-day version: "Whatever you just put in the chat, I want you to have done by Friday" (`IT/...5 day challenge.md` ~01:11).
- **Day 3:** "anything goes", a wins reel, advanced demos, then the offer.

**Run-of-show templates.**
- May 30 2026 Day 3 (`K/sales/day3-game-plan-the-close.md`), timed blocks: warm open (5) → finish a live build promised the day before (20, "It pays off the Day 2… cliffhanger, which makes the whole boot camp feel like one designed story") → emotional peak (7) → promised deliverable (18) → deep dive (25) → biggest live capability demo (35) → clean offer (30) → close (20) → Q&A as objection handling.
- 3-Day AI MBA: "the MBA concept in five minutes, AI does it in fifteen, an artifact on the screen, one proof line in the chat. Watch, Do, Prove." Each day has a "Finish line" sentence (`K/products/13-days-to-done/3-day-ai-mba-curriculum.md`).
- Bonus-session agenda: minute-by-minute 60-minute core, an optional 30-minute studio with "three lanes only", and facilitator scripts for switching ideas and feature creep (`PB/2026-07-30-build-lab-bonus-session-agenda.md`).

**Room craft (from his seminar years, `IT/2024-01-17-stage-close-and-event-flow.md`; `IT/2024-03-11-mfm-debrief-event-and-feedback.md`).**
- "The event doesn't start at 8:30, the event starts in the lobby."
- Never self-introduce; use an emcee who edifies the speaker.
- Pattern-interrupt at the open.
- "Bring everyone in at once with energy."
- Vary pace: "A confused mind always says no", so pre-empt confusion on Day 1 ("you'll get confused, that's okay").
- "Lead with the strongest content piece."
- Visible transitions between topics.
- Restore energy after lunch.

**Radical transparency as a sales method (Lanny's signature).** "Anytime I'm manipulating you, or doing something, which I'm gonna be doing a lot of, I'm gonna tell you about it, because there's gonna be, like, radical transparency" (`ZT/2026-07-27-2057-...` line ~92). The audit lists it as something to protect (§8.7).

**Roles.**
- Planned: "Lanny Founder · Closer · Stage… [name removed] Ops Lead · Qualification… OS Cortex Concierge… Day-before show-up messages… Harv Eker / SoMM Team List & Distribution" (strategy page).
- In the room: "Have [name removed] drop the signup link in chat repeatedly" (`K/sales/day3-game-plan-the-close.md`). Volunteer community members ran support and links (audit §8.8).
- Seminar version: the speaker works the floor in waves, admins process, and reps convert the "hangers" (`K/training/event-floor-closing-system.md`; in-person only).
- Lanny's own debrief self-criticism: "I didn't do a good enough job of edifying haley" (`ZT/...2026-08-05...` 00:56:56).

**Chat.** 1,305 chat messages in July (audit §0). Mine the chat for names and builds to call on. Use "Screenshot this one" lines to signal value and move chat (`K/sales/day3-game-plan-the-close.md`). Read aggregate pre-work data back to the room: "308 people with truth/integrity as a core value" (audit §1).

**Testimonials on Day 2.**
- Bring a prior success on live, in their own words. a paid-program member appeared on Day 2, "the campaign kept its biggest promise", and Lanny "walked her entire funnel on the whiteboard with real numbers" (audit §2 Day 2).
- Frame it honestly: "this is not a testimonial… I want you to see what it looks like to take this and execute" (proof bank §D). Live: "that sounds an awful lot like a testimonial that I might have been fishing for, and I wasn't" (`ZT/2026-07-28-2100-...` 01:31:53).
- On-air rules for featured attendees: "featured people had to email you AND stay to the end of Day 2" (`K/sales/day3-game-plan-the-close.md`).
- Day-3 recap asset: an AI-generated "AI-Lanny" video built overnight from the Day 1–2 transcripts, carrying the scoreboard and the pitch. "The best single asset produced this year" (audit §2 Day 3; Lanny explains the prompt at `ZT/2026-07-29-2100-...` 00:26:56).
- Testimonial quality rule: "I met THIS person, did THIS deal, here are the financials, here's how my life changed… Not 'I had a great time'" (`IT/up-event-launch-campaign-decomposition.md`).
- Vetting: never feature someone who has spun off a competing company (same file).

### 2.6 CLOSE: offer tiers, stage close, payment terms, VIP upsell, applications

**Offer shape on stage (Lanny's 12-step).** Reason why → big benefit → "just for you" → walkthrough → price → bonus 1 → risk reversal → best bonus → recap → best bonus again → guarantee again (`K/copywriting/offer-structure-12-step-template.md`). Taught live on Sep 20: "The listener who is still deciding is deciding on exactly two things. What's the best thing I can get, and what happens if it doesn't work?" (`ZT/2026-09-20-Built-for-Freedom-...txt`).

**"Which one are you" ladder.** "People buy when they know which door is theirs" (`K/sales/day3-game-plan-the-close.md` Gap 2). Also: "State the annual savings on EVERY tier", and use a "Day Zero map… free seat, VIP, liftoff, build room, 4 columns" so buyers can see what they already own (`K/events/2026-09-20-liftoff-offer-workshop-prep.md` §6; Sep 20 transcript).

**Honest act-now reason.** "The bonus session as the act-now reason… This is your honest deadline. It's real, it's valuable, it's time-bound" (`K/sales/day3-game-plan-the-close.md`). July: "Live buyer bonus expires tonight at midnight" (`ZT/2026-07-29-2100-...`). The audit criticises July for never using "The cohort date… a real deadline" (§5 Day 3).

**Risk reversal.**
- July Day 2: "if you don't think it was worth, I would say, 10 times what you paid, I would demand that you request a refund" (`ZT/2026-07-28-2100-...` line ~656).
- Liftoff rebuild: "if by day 90, nobody has paid you, we keep working with you free until someone does" (Sep 20 transcript).
- Seminar version: "A money-back guarantee increases conversions… a strong product gets very few refunds" (`IT/2024-01-17-...`).

**Takeaway / exclusivity close.** "If you're a jerk, if you yell at waiters… please don't join… But if you're the kind of person who showed up for three days and stayed to the end, you're exactly who we built this for." The one line that closes: "You've spent three days proving to yourself you can do this. The only question left is whether you do it alone, or with us." Then "Don't talk past the close" (`K/sales/day3-game-plan-the-close.md`).

**App-delivered close (July experiment).** A personalized AI-Lanny video → $1 Build Brief → one-click $997. "The close had been fully delegated to the application", with "no offer stack presentation, no scarcity, no deadline" (audit §2 Day 3).

**Payment terms.**
- Offer a plan, never a deeper discount. "Payment arrangements are offered rather than discounted further" (`K/sales/objection-handling-stage-close.md`, Natoli).
- "Meet them on terms, not price… Hold the headline number… Personal delivery closes it… the payment link goes out the same day as the approval" (`K/sales/meet-them-on-terms-playbook.md`).
- Plans must not punish: "3 × $497 and 12 × $197 cost 50% and 137% more than pay-in-full. That reads as a penalty" (`K/events/2026-09-20-...` §6). Live fix: "$997, or 3 payments of $349. $50 for the convenience, not $500" (Sep 20 transcript).

**VIP upsell.** "Once the sale is locked and you have the credit card, offer a VIP upsell as a simple checkbox, not a re-sell… never confuse the primary sale with the upsell" (`IT/2024-01-17-...`).

## 4. Lessons and failure patterns (what broke)

1. **Partner-fronted promise vs who actually teaches.** All 16 emails were first-person Harv, including "I'll see you there". Five minutes into Day 1: "I thought T. Harv Eker was presenting this." Lanny: "I'd probably be like, oh my god, I've been duped." The rule existed in the playbook and was violated in the shipped emails (audit §1.1; `PB/july-2026-bootcamp-invitation-sequence.md` "Verified ground truth"). **Skill rule:** the invitation must name who teaches live.
2. **Proof inflation.** Email 9 turned a paid-program member's account ("I was charging $15,000, and so I charged $25,000, and in this 2 weeks, I made $30,000") into "two conversations… a $25,000 check". She was also a paid-program member (audit §1.2; proof bank §D). **Skill rule:** classify proof first, then use the speaker's own words plus an honest qualifier: "Are you going to make $30,000 because you completed these two steps? No. Probably not."
3. **Unkept or contradictory promises in pre-event emails.** Email 5 said "something is coming" and there is no evidence it shipped. Email 6 said "not a sales call" for calls routed toward enrollment. The promo said "25 minutes" when the median was 48.7 (audit §1.3–1.4; followup-redesign §6).
4. **Reminder misfires on consecutive days.** The Day 2 "we're live" email fired ~4 hours early, twice; Day 3 opened at 82 people. "I said, hey, let's make sure it's right for day 3… And it happened again" (`ZT/...2026-08-05...` 00:34:42; audit §2). **Fix:** "A tested schedule with a dry-run 24h before" (audit §5).
5. **No Day-1-night or Day-2-night email** (audit §1.5).
6. **Show-up gates create friction.** The FOREVERFREE access code blocked named attendees on camera and was removed live about 90 minutes in. A 20-hour delay on a paid deliverable needed manual overrides (audit §2 Day 1b, 1d).
7. **Untested live tech.**
   - First-ever breakout rooms cost about 45 minutes: "I have trauma. I need EMDR because of those breakout rooms" (audit §2 Day 1a).
   - Email-in-URL caused real data loss when links were forwarded (Day 1c).
   - The app was self-hosted on office internet and went down on Day 2 (audit §2 Day 2).
   - A live song app returned silence at 45 concurrent users, and Lanny told the room "Please don't buy the $30 video" (audit §2 Day 3).
8. **Checkout broken at peak intent at two consecutive camps,** and nobody could buy in September. Feb: "duplicate order forms, a broken order bump". Jul: Stripe "you may only specify one of these parameters, customer or customer_email". The warning sign on Day 2 was ignored. Sep: "I built a door… and forgot to unlock it" (audit §2 Day 3; `K/events/2026-09-20-...` §0).
9. **No structured close; confused offers.**
   - July Day 3 had "no deadline, no recap… no call to action".
   - The 100-app bonus read as bloat: "what do we need all of that for?" / "You probably don't."
   - In September people didn't know what Liftoff was ("I don't know exactly what's in it", Lanny, Day 2). One attendee asked to be pitched the Build Room, which undercut the $997 offer.
   - Plans that punish, and two contradictory guarantees (audit §2 Day 3; `K/events/2026-09-20-...` §3, §6).
10. **Slowing down ready buyers** by forcing them through every phase before they could pay (`ZT/...2026-08-05...` 00:46:13).
11. **Follow-up engine switched off.** 0 of 10 workflows were published, 2,181 enrollments frozen, and approval was stale on a reply that had already arrived. "The entire post-event conversion window, spent" (audit §3–4). Only buyers got fast follow-up.
12. **Single-point-of-failure sending.** The partner controlled the sending and the reminders ("I didn't handle these emails, Harv's team did"). Earlier, CRM import gaps left people off sequences and invites landed in spam (`ZT/...2026-08-05...`; `K/meetings/forever-free-bootcamp/2026-04-13.md`; `PB/july-2026-harv-eker-campaign.md` "Sending is the single point of failure").
13. **Post-purchase access failures** are "the most commercially expensive pattern": replay and Zoom links hand-delivered, wrong ZIPs, stuck deployments (`FB/patterns.md`; `FB/dashboard-wins-losses.md`).
14. **Undocumented prices and terms.** "Eight distinct price/term shapes were quoted to customers in fourteen days"; an approved plan went 11+ days without a payment link (`K/sales/meet-them-on-terms-playbook.md`; `FB/knowledge-gaps.md`).
15. **No program calendar or replay index.** Dates were inferred from inbound email ("Build Lab started Monday 2026-08-10", inferred). The Sep 10–12 bootcamp was "recorded nowhere canonical" (`FB/knowledge-gaps.md`; `FB/MONTHLY-REVIEW.md`).
16. **Proof-free follow-up emails.** "Zero proof across all 32 emails… while a verification-audited proof bank sits unused" (audit §5).
17. **Self-defeating subject lines** such as "A clear no is allowed" and "I'll leave this with you" (audit §5).
18. **Seminar-era lessons.**
    - Don't send people to lunch with an unfilled application.
    - "Some negatives backfire": a "don't trust the internet / BBB" video cost sales.
    - Back-table staff chatting devalues the speaker.
    - Never run the back-table rush mid-presentation.
    - Traffic concentration on one channel, with ad learning reset per city, is a risk (`IT/2024-03-11-...`; `K/training/event-floor-closing-system.md`).
19. **What worked (protect).** Pre-event self-serve tool; first-party app (credited with the retention gain); personalized outputs (a named attendee's own project); the $1 commitment device; the AI-Lanny Day-3 video; radical transparency; community volunteers; the founder as instructor; the age-as-advantage frame (audit §8; planning brief §8).

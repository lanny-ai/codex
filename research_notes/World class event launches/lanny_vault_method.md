# Lanny Morton's event-launch method, distilled from the vault

Research notes for the Build Room OS "Event Launcher" skill. Source: the founder's private notes, which are not in this repository. Compiled 2026-10-17.

Evidence base: seven runs of Lanny's own free 3-day "Forever Free AI Boot Camp" (Jul 2025, Nov 2025, Dec 2025, Jan 2026, Feb 2026, Mar 2026, May 2026, Jul 2026), an Apr 2026 bonus day, the Sep 18–20 2026 "Built for Freedom" 3-day intensive, a "Maximum Productivity 5-Day Challenge", the post-camp Build Lab cohort, and his earlier real-estate seminar business (with two former seminar-business partners). The July 2026 camp is by far the best documented. It has a full audit, a data analysis, Lanny's own debrief to members, and all three day transcripts (founder's notes).

---

## 1. What matters most (10 lines)

1. **The event is the accelerant, not the funnel.** Put a free, self-serve "first win" tool (the Navigator, which produces a Compass) in front of the event and get people to finish it before Day 1. A few hundred Compasses were done before doors opened, at about 92% completion (founder's notes).
2. **Install one belief before you invite.** Three belief emails (opportunity, identity, mechanism), then an invitation that only sells the free seat and live attendance. Never sell the offer in the invitation (founder's notes).
3. **Live building, not lecture.** Every attendee produces something on Day 1. "A total stranger, often 70+ and anti-tech, walked in scared and walked out with a plan / website / first sale" (founder's notes).
4. **Expect decay and engineer show-up on every day, not just Day 1.** July went from under a thousand registered to roughly a third of them live on Day 1, then a steady decline to a few dozen at the close. The Day 2 and Day 3 reminders misfired, and no Day-1-night or Day-2-night email existed (founder's notes).
5. **A tiny first transaction changes the relationship.** The one-dollar Build Brief (about a hundred buyers) made the core offer a one-click upsell. Lanny's figures: "One-click upsells can convert at… 20, 30, 40, 50%" vs "3-5%" when people must re-enter details (founder's notes).
6. **Proof must be honest and classified.** Free-camp wins are separated from paid-member and external results (classes A–E). The one integrity breach in July (a paid member's five-figure result presented as a free-camp win) is called out in the audit (founder's notes).
7. **Test every checkout path on the morning of the close day.** Checkout broke at peak intent at two camps in a row (Feb: order forms and order bump; Jul: Stripe identity collision). In September nobody could buy at all (founder's notes).
8. **Decide deliberately whether the close day has a human close.** July had "zero" structured close and still converted about one in five of the dollar-buyers. Nobody tested what a 20-minute structured close would have added (founder's notes).
9. **Follow-up is where the money leaks.** All ten lifecycle workflows sat in draft after the camp, with a couple of thousand enrollments frozen. Dozens of people paid the dollar and never saw the core offer, even though about 56% of those who did see the price bought (founder's notes).
10. **Write every price, term, date and link down before the event.** Undocumented custom terms, missing replay links and inferred cohort dates kept costing money and trust (founder's notes).

## 2. Lanny's method as a timeline

### 2.1 DECIDE: offer, the one belief, event format

**Format.** The format is a free, live, multi-day Zoom event with a value-first arc that ends in a closing-day offer. "Day 1 ~3h, Day 2 ~3h, Day 3 'anything goes' (runs long, the close)". Replays run about 7 days (founder's notes). The format held across runs, and only the tooling evolved: "The format is stable across runs; the tooling and Day-2/3 polish evolve" (founder's notes). Variants tested:
- 3-day free boot camp (core).
- 5-day challenge with a sprint promise: "The promise of this program was make five… five dollars, five days, five thousand… What I wanted you to discover is… a gear that you never knew you had" (founder's notes).
- Paid 3-day intensive with VIP seats (Sep 2026 "Built for Freedom" / "3-Day AI MBA": a low-four-figure seat price, two seats riding with every copy of a low-priced Personal Edition, and VIP seats at a mid-three-figure price; founder's notes).
- Post-sale "live buyer bonus" session (founder's notes).

**Pick the format by where the sale happens.** "Webinar funnels (Pattern A) end in a booked call. Live-event funnels (Pattern B) end in butts in seats for a stage close. Pick the pattern by where the actual sale happens" (founder's notes).

**The one belief (Lanny calls it the "big domino", a term he borrows from Brunson).** Write it as one sentence. July's version: "AI is the single biggest wealth leverage of my lifetime, the window to grab it is open right now, and it works for someone like me…" (founder's notes). Under it sit three supporting beliefs, each replacing one false belief (opportunity / identity / mechanism). Sequence logic: "want it (1), then qualify yourself in (2), then believe it is doable (3)."
- Reframe rule: "Sell the freedom, not the technology… The moment it smells like homework, we lose them."
- Urgency rule: "Do not manufacture fake urgency. The real 'left behind' fear is enough."

**The offer ladder (decide before you promote).**
- Planned July ladder: the core offer (high three figures) → Build Room (low four figures) → a five-figure yearly Platinum tier (founder's notes). The same notes added "Every dollar paid at any level credits toward the next" and a Day-3 "48-Hour Buy Now Special" bundle.
- Actual July ladder: Compass (free) → Blueprint (free) → **one-dollar Build Brief** → **Build Lab at the core program price, high three figures, pay in full or a twelve-month plan** → Build Room (the high-ticket tier, four figures) → Complete Year (high four figures) (founder's notes, "offer stack as actually sold").
- Lesson: the ladder changed between plan and event, and no canonical price-ladder document exists (founder's notes).

**Free-front-end logic.** "Free front-end logic modeled on Vistaprint (give the front end, monetize the ascension)" (founder's notes). The promotion partner's rule: "The headaches are in the front end. The money is in the back end… You've got to have the back end to support it" (founder's notes). This is the partner's stage-selling IP; see §5.

**Offer quality checklist (Lanny's own vault distillation).** "One person, one problem… A specific promise, denominated in something they can hold… Bonuses kill objections… A guarantee that carries the risk… Honest urgency and scarcity… Name it like a result" (founder's notes). It draws on Hormozi and an offer coach; see §5.

### 2.2 BUILD: pages, calendar, reminders, proof

**Assets list (transferable).**
- From a real-estate author's evergreen funnel: "Lead magnet, indoctrination page + video… Waitlist page, expiring pages, countdown timers… Webinar invite + reminder emails + reminder SMS… Objection-handling and countdown sequences… cart-abandon sequence, one-click upsell… recordings-only offer for non-buyers, payment-plan order form… Customer-experience onboarding" (founder's notes).
- The promotion partner's challenge page inventory (structure only): invite page (list and FB) → TY page with OTO for the live event → separate final TY pages for "YES VIP Pass" and "NO VIP Pass" → daily Facebook Live per day → sales page with bonuses → cart → replay page (founder's notes).
- The partner's webclass inventory: reg / TY / pre-training 1 and 2 / recap / replay / "Replay Non-Attendee" / expired-offer / waitlist / encore reg pages, plus a URL naming convention with "/green" for evergreen variants (founder's notes).

**Personalized first-win tool.** In July the Navigator (22 questions) produced a Compass. The Architect then produced a Blueprint, and a dollar bought a Build Brief. "Each phase was designed to deliver standalone value, not just advance towards a sale" (Lanny, founder's notes).
- Why first-party matters: Lanny moved the tools from custom GPTs to his own web app because "everybody didn't have paid ChatGPT" (an attendee, quoted in the founder's notes). The move also gave him data on every user, where "with custom GPTs… the only thing you can see is how many people, approximately" (Lanny, founder's notes).
- Identity capture: the promo link embedded the email in the URL "so… I had a record of every single person's interaction from the very beginning". This later caused data loss when people forwarded links (founder's notes). See §4.

**Greased slide in every form.** "The first question has to be slippery… Resistance is highest when a process first starts" (founder's notes). Data check: about 30% of starters quit on question 2, a heavy values question placed right after the easy warmup (founder's notes). The same rule applies to challenge opt-ins: "If you ask for first name, last name, email, choose country, mobile phone, a checkbox… nobody's gonna fill that out… why not just put them right into the challenge right there?" (founder's notes).

**Calendar files.**
- One .ics file with one VEVENT per day, each with its own summary ("Forever Free Boot Camp - Day 1/2/3"), the join link, and a pre-work reminder on Day 1 ("Bring your Compass") (founder's notes).
- AddEvent widget: "+ Add All 3 Days to Calendar", `RRULE FREQ=DAILY;COUNT=3`, timezone `America/Los_Angeles`, 15-minute alarm, a per-contact merge field for the Zoom link (`{{ contact.zoom_event_join_link }}`), and a day-by-day promise in the description (founder's notes).
- Email-safe HTML button block that links to the hosted widget (founder's notes).
- Timezone discipline: "Stated as '2:00 PM Pacific Standard Time,' but late July is Pacific DAYLIGHT time… Copy says '2:00 PM Pacific'" (founder's notes). The confirmation email lists PT/MT/CT/ET/London and a converter link (founder's notes, Confirmation email).

**Proof bank before copy.** Classify every story: "(A) FREE-CAMP WIN — transformation… (B) FREE-CAMP WIN — cash… (C) FREEDOM / IMPACT… (D) PAID-PROGRAM WIN… NOT usable as free-camp proof… (E) EXTERNALLY-CITED EXAMPLE… (U) UNCERTAIN" (founder's notes). Rules: "Do not dress a paid-member or external number as a free-camp attendee result"; "DON'T OVERSTATE PIPELINE AS CLOSED." The pre-event evidence audit was flagged as a gap and never done before July (founder's notes).

**Ops pre-flight (from what broke).** Host the app on cloud infrastructure, not office internet. Dry-run the reminder schedule 24 hours before. Test checkout "with a real card, from a non-US IP, on the morning of Day 3". Rehearse breakout rooms or don't use them (founder's notes, "What automation replaces"). The bonus-session host checklist adds: "Name one person to watch chat and one person to capture unresolved blockers… End with the exact next action visible on screen" (founder's notes).

### 2.3 FILL: invitation sequence, segments, partners

**Sequence shape (Lanny's default skeleton, used across many funnels).**
- Pattern A, webinar to appointment: "Pre-Frame Story Sequence (3 emails, 1 day apart)… Invitation Sequence (5 emails, 1 day apart)… Reminder Sequence (3 messages)… No-Show Sequence (2 messages)". Branches: registered vs not, attended vs no-show (founder's notes).
- Pattern B, live event: adds "Multi-channel retargeting… A Video Sales Letter asset… for non-openers… Countdown / Closeout Sequence (3 emails)".
- "Do not lead with the register link. Earn the click with the pre-frame."

**July 2026 as actually shipped (16 emails, all from the promotion partner in first person; founder's notes).**
- **Phase 1 (Jul 13–19), 7 daily emails at 9:00 AM PT.** "No boot camp mention until Email 7. The only CTA in Emails 1–6 is the free Navigator". Arc: founder story ("He was making great money. And he resented every dollar of it.") → why → "This is not a personality test" (concede the objection, "probably nothing", then reframe to utility) → give away one actual question → personalized page delivered → 15-minute Compass Debrief calls → Email 7, "Something's happening July 27th — wanted you to know first." The audit called this "the single best-performing asset in this entire funnel."
- **Phase 2 (Jul 20–26), 7 daily invitation emails.** Day-by-day → anchor proof story → "You may have seen me promote this before" (repeat-attendee objection) → "Three days is a lot" (time objection plus value math) → "Is this the right event for you?" (disqualification: "Lanny's room is not a classroom. It's a workshop… on Day 2, you are going to try to sell something") → fence-sitters → final word.
- **Event-day emails:** 6:00 AM "Today is the day", then 1:45 PM "15 minutes." with the join link.
- **Confirmation email** sent immediately: join link, three per-day add-to-calendar links, a timezone table, and one pre-work action (finish the Navigator).

**Earlier invitation draft rules (founder's notes).**
- "Every CTA is the same: save the free seat, then show up live. One decision, repeated."
- Tease the close-day offer only: "an offer I have never made before."
- "Why free" answered honestly: "at the end, a small number of you are going to want to go further with me. That is how this works."
- "Do not plan to 'just catch the replay'… The replay watchers mean well, and most of them never press play."

**Segments.**
- List temperature tiers (hot/warm/cool/cold × business/consumer). The cold list gets eight value-only emails with engagement scoring: open 1, click 3, reply 6, self-segment reply 8, hand-raise keyword 10 (founder's notes).
- Reply prompts at the end of every belief email "self-segment" readers by temperature, objection and demand (founder's notes, Engagement notes).
- Deliverability: "Send from a subdomain… Ramp volume… Sunset policy" (cold nurture). The Apr 2026 bonus day showed what happens without it: "Surprise there's only [a handful] people… did the email… go into spam?" (founder's notes).
- Speed to contact for the top tier: "iMessage from Lanny within 10 minutes of list entry (Tier A only)" (founder's notes).

**Partners / JV.**
- Lanny's own top lever historically: "getting JV partners to promote my offer and getting 'em to promote it more". He approached them by leading with a give: "I'm looking for offers to promote. I have a mailing list of…" (founder's notes).
- Partner vetting by EPC: "what's your earnings per click, and do you have screenshots to back it up?" (founder's notes).
- Partner-sent link mechanics: "[the promotion partner] is an affiliate… they send emails" (founder's notes).
- Affiliate rules from his earlier real-estate seminar business: "Pay affiliates after the event… let them choose cash or product… require the affiliate to be a registered attendee" (founder's notes).
- Partner-fronted promotion risk: see §4 (the partner bait-and-switch moment).

**Decompose a big launch into owned campaigns.** "If three people say they can work on it, it usually never gets done unless somebody takes responsibility." Campaigns: room-block, affiliate, keynote/speaker, internal database, external ads, sponsorship. Run a KPI dashboard per campaign (founder's notes).

### 2.4 SHOW-UP ENGINEERING: calendar files, reminders, day-before content

- **Pre-work makes showing up valuable.** "If you've done the Navigator and have your Compass, you arrive with a head start" (Email 8). "Day 1 starts where previous boot camps ended" (Email 10) (founder's notes).
- **The day before:** a "how to prepare" email. "Show up live… Be at a computer, not just your phone… Come ready to actually do it" (founder's notes, Email 6).
- **Day-of:** morning email, then a 15-minute email (founder's notes, Emails 15–16). The real-estate author's funnel uses a "15 minute" SMS. Indoctrination emails between registration and the event "pre-sell the idea and the presenter so they show up primed" (founder's notes).
- **Missing in July and named as the cheapest win:** "a Day-1-night recap plus Day-2 tease, and a Day-2-night 'tomorrow is the one'" (founder's notes).
- **Planned show-up hook:** "On Day 1 live, we run your Compass through the Architect… Can't happen from the recording." Target "65%+ of registrants (past average was ~33%)" (founder's notes). Actual was about 40%.
- **What did NOT work: gating content to force live attendance.** "I did the access code that you have to show up live to get it, as a way to get people to show up" (founder's notes). He removed it live: "the access code was just deployed to get people to show up anyway, so why don't we just remove that part". Debrief: "That gate didn't work as well as I hoped… It just created friction" (founder's notes).
- **Lanny's day-over-day rule of thumb:** "Day 1 is always high. Day 2 is always about, you know, 80% or so of day… one, and then… probably another 80% show up on day 3" (founder's notes). Note the conflict: the audit says prior camps ran "roughly 50% Day 1→Day 2". See §3.
- **Reward showing up publicly.** The Day 2 open reads out the founding dollar-buyers (a few dozen) by name. Day 3: "you're one of the [few hundred still here], and I want to acknowledge you for that" (founder's notes).

### 2.5 RUN: day-by-day arc, run-of-show, roles, chat, testimonials on Day 2

**Arc.** "Belief on Day 1. Proof on Day 2. Ascension on Day 3" (founder's notes). The confirmation email's version: "Day 1 is clarity. Day 2 is revenue. Day 3 is momentum. Missing one is like leaving at halftime" (founder's notes).
- **Day 1:** belief first ("If you don't believe that it's possible, then it won't happen"), then a voice-driven personal tool and a near-universal "holy crap" moment. Rule: "Move one guaranteed-win build into the first 20 minutes" (founder's notes).
- **Day 2:** put it to work with a timed action challenge ("sell a custom app in 17 minutes" produced a dozen live sales in Jul 2025; founder's notes) and the economics teaching ("acquisition cost must be less than day-one revenue"). 5-day version: "Whatever you just put in the chat, I want you to have done by Friday" (founder's notes).
- **Day 3:** "anything goes", a wins reel, advanced demos, then the offer.

**Run-of-show templates.**
- May 30 2026 Day 3 (founder's notes), timed blocks: warm open (5) → finish a live build promised the day before (20, "It pays off the Day 2… cliffhanger, which makes the whole boot camp feel like one designed story") → emotional peak (7) → promised deliverable (18) → deep dive (25) → biggest live capability demo (35) → clean offer (30) → close (20) → Q&A as objection handling.
- 3-Day AI MBA: "the MBA concept in five minutes, AI does it in fifteen, an artifact on the screen, one proof line in the chat. Watch, Do, Prove." Each day has a "Finish line" sentence (founder's notes).
- Bonus-session agenda: minute-by-minute 60-minute core, an optional 30-minute studio with "three lanes only", and facilitator scripts for switching ideas and feature creep (founder's notes).

**Room craft (from his seminar years; founder's notes).**
- "The event doesn't start at 8:30, the event starts in the lobby."
- Never self-introduce; use an emcee who edifies the speaker.
- Pattern-interrupt at the open.
- "Bring everyone in at once with energy."
- Vary pace: "A confused mind always says no", so pre-empt confusion on Day 1 ("you'll get confused, that's okay").
- "Lead with the strongest content piece."
- Visible transitions between topics.
- Restore energy after lunch.

**Radical transparency as a sales method (Lanny's signature).** "Anytime I'm manipulating you, or doing something, which I'm gonna be doing a lot of, I'm gonna tell you about it, because there's gonna be, like, radical transparency" (founder's notes). The audit lists it as something to protect.

**Roles.**
- Planned: "Lanny Founder · Closer · Stage… [name removed] Ops Lead · Qualification… OS Cortex Concierge… Day-before show-up messages… [the promotion partner's team] List & Distribution" (founder's notes).
- In the room: "Have [name removed] drop the signup link in chat repeatedly" (founder's notes). Volunteer community members ran support and links.
- Seminar version: the speaker works the floor in waves, admins process, and reps convert the "hangers" (founder's notes; in-person only).
- Lanny's own debrief self-criticism: "I didn't do a good enough job of edifying [name removed]" (founder's notes).

**Chat.** Over a thousand chat messages in July (founder's notes). Mine the chat for names and builds to call on. Use "Screenshot this one" lines to signal value and move chat. Read aggregate pre-work data back to the room: "[a few hundred] people with truth/integrity as a core value" (founder's notes).

**Testimonials on Day 2.**
- Bring a prior success on live, in their own words. A paid-program member appeared on Day 2, "the campaign kept its biggest promise", and Lanny "walked her entire funnel on the whiteboard with real numbers" (founder's notes).
- Frame it honestly: "this is not a testimonial… I want you to see what it looks like to take this and execute" (founder's notes). Live: "that sounds an awful lot like a testimonial that I might have been fishing for, and I wasn't" (founder's notes).
- On-air rules for featured attendees: "featured people had to email you AND stay to the end of Day 2" (founder's notes).
- Day-3 recap asset: an AI-generated "AI-Lanny" video built overnight from the Day 1–2 transcripts, carrying the scoreboard and the pitch. "The best single asset produced this year" (founder's notes; Lanny explains the prompt on Day 3).
- Testimonial quality rule: "I met THIS person, did THIS deal, here are the financials, here's how my life changed… Not 'I had a great time'" (founder's notes).
- Vetting: never feature someone who has spun off a competing company.

### 2.6 CLOSE: offer tiers, stage close, payment terms, VIP upsell, applications

**Offer shape on stage (Lanny's 12-step).** Reason why → big benefit → "just for you" → walkthrough → price → bonus 1 → risk reversal → best bonus → recap → best bonus again → guarantee again (founder's notes). Taught live on Sep 20: "The listener who is still deciding is deciding on exactly two things. What's the best thing I can get, and what happens if it doesn't work?" (founder's notes).

**"Which one are you" ladder.** "People buy when they know which door is theirs" (founder's notes). Also: "State the annual savings on EVERY tier", and use a "Day Zero map… free seat, VIP, liftoff, build room, 4 columns" so buyers can see what they already own (founder's notes).

**Honest act-now reason.** "The bonus session as the act-now reason… This is your honest deadline. It's real, it's valuable, it's time-bound" (founder's notes). July: "Live buyer bonus expires tonight at midnight" (founder's notes). The audit criticises July for never using "The cohort date… a real deadline".

**Risk reversal.**
- July Day 2: "if you don't think it was worth, I would say, 10 times what you paid, I would demand that you request a refund" (founder's notes).
- Liftoff rebuild: "if by day 90, nobody has paid you, we keep working with you free until someone does" (founder's notes).
- Seminar version: "A money-back guarantee increases conversions… a strong product gets very few refunds" (founder's notes).

**Takeaway / exclusivity close.** "If you're a jerk, if you yell at waiters… please don't join… But if you're the kind of person who showed up for three days and stayed to the end, you're exactly who we built this for." The one line that closes: "You've spent three days proving to yourself you can do this. The only question left is whether you do it alone, or with us." Then "Don't talk past the close" (founder's notes).

**App-delivered close (July experiment).** A personalized AI-Lanny video → one-dollar Build Brief → one-click core offer. "The close had been fully delegated to the application", with "no offer stack presentation, no scarcity, no deadline" (founder's notes).

**Payment terms.**
- Offer a plan, never a deeper discount. "Payment arrangements are offered rather than discounted further" (founder's notes, from the seminar business).
- "Meet them on terms, not price… Hold the headline number… Personal delivery closes it… the payment link goes out the same day as the approval" (founder's notes).
- Plans must not punish: the planned three-pay and twelve-pay plans cost half again and well over double the pay-in-full price, which "reads as a penalty" (founder's notes). Live fix: pay in full, or three payments that add a small convenience fee, "for the convenience, not" a penalty (founder's notes).

**VIP upsell.** "Once the sale is locked and you have the credit card, offer a VIP upsell as a simple checkbox, not a re-sell… never confuse the primary sale with the upsell" (founder's notes).
- The promotion partner's challenges ran VIP passes with separate VIP reminders and VIP Q&A calls (founder's notes).
- Lanny's VIP spec for a paid intensive: "Front-row hot seat each day… every recording and transcript plus the prompts and files… a private 30-minute build review… first access… at the graduate rate" (founder's notes).
- July held VIP/replay tiers: "VIP replay tier: include in invitation or hold for on-camp only (currently held)" (founder's notes).
- Order bumps: Lanny: "My sixth love language is… OTOs and order bumps" (founder's notes).

**Applications (seminar heritage).** "Fill out the application and turn it in whether you think you can do it or not. It's up to me to figure out how to get you from point A to point B." "Never send people to lunch with an unfilled application" (founder's notes). Online analogue: the 15-minute Compass Debrief and strategy sessions. Be honest that these are sales-adjacent; the audit flags Email 6's "not a sales call" claim (founder's notes).

**Let ready buyers buy.** "There were people that were like… forget the rest of the process, I want it into this program… here I am slowing them down from giving money, which was, like… Dumb… when they heard a paid-program member… that would have been a good time to… say, here… put your credit card" (founder's notes).

### 2.7 FOLLOW UP: attended / no-show / bought / didn't, replay, encore

**Segment by state, not by "attended".**
- The production data shows nine real segments, e.g. "Compass complete, never opened the Architect" (several hundred), "Build Brief ready, never bought Build Lab" (about a hundred), "one-dollar checkout abandoned" (a few dozen, "no workflow at all") (founder's notes).
- Rule: "Don't restart the pitch. Restart the work… one email, one person's exact next step, one link" (founder's notes).

**No-shows.** "Give them the Compass directly… Then send the member funnel segment, not a 4h46m replay… Do not send 'sorry you missed it, here's the replay.' That framing makes them someone who failed. Send the tool" (founder's notes).

**Attended, didn't buy (three-day loyalists).** Find out the reason (money / capability doubt / timing): "the cohort calendar converts 'not now' into 'October'." For the few dozen most engaged, send a personal email from Lanny "referencing something they actually said" (founder's notes).

**Hot recoveries are human and same-day.** "The Day 3 checkout victims… should get a personal email from Lanny today, naming what happened, apologising, and carrying a working link" (founder's notes). The P0 list ("no QA required") is in the founder's notes.

**Re-engagement shape.** "Five emails… ten days: the artifact… proof… the objection… the deadline… the clean close ('I'll stop asking about this' — then actually stop)" (founder's notes).

**Timing.** "FF01 currently fires at 24 hours… People who are going to finish, finish in under an hour… Move FF01a step 1 to 2–3 hours" (founder's notes).
- Loss-log example: "Send replay within 4 hours" (founder's notes). This is an illustrative template entry, not a dated loss; label it as such.

**Replay.**
- Window: about 7 days ("Recordings vanish after 7 days", invitation Email 6). The founder's notes planned "72-hour replay with the cart open" and "Scoop-Up Closes".
- July replay assets: "Day 2 replay campaign… Day 3 replay page — the 4h46m recording cut to a verified 1h58m conversion edit… with both payment options, the guarantee" (founder's notes).
- MBA plan: "Replays cut per day within 12 hours; the replay email is already written."
- Turn recordings into SOPs: "All the sessions will be recorded, transcribed, and turned into easy-to-follow standard operating procedure systems" (founder's notes).
- Gap: there is no canonical replay-link index, which causes weekly "where's the replay" tickets (founder's notes).

**Encore / evergreen.**
- The promotion partner ran encore webinars with "Encore Webinar Reminder Email to Show Up" and "Encore Webinar Non Buyer Emails" (founder's notes).
- Lanny's evergreen plan: "traffic → Compass → Blueprint → one-dollar Build Brief → Build Lab. Always on. The camp fires quarterly"; "A published rolling cohort calendar. Every 2–4 weeks" (founder's notes).
- A later plan (founder's notes): "A monthly 2-hour Demo Day replaces 3-day boot camps."

**Buyers.** Run a live buyer bonus within 24 hours, with the invitation "from Lanny's Gmail" the same afternoon to every active paid buyer. Send personalized welcome videos (a couple of dozen in July) (founder's notes).
- Access is the acceptance test: "A sale is delivered when the customer has independently reached the thing, not when it is paid" (founder's notes).
- Every custom term goes into a client note the same day (founder's notes).

### 2.8 DEBRIEF

**Method (Lanny-run, from his seminar business).** "Reframe feedback as a gift… separate 'what worked' from 'what could be better' into two passes… Have the person receiving feedback self-assess first… Push for specifics" (founder's notes).

**Artefacts Lanny produced for July (a template for the skill):**
- A data-backed audit with the funnel table, revenue table, "conversion leaks, ranked", per-stage improvements and "what's working — protect these" (founder's notes).
- A production-data analysis of drop-off by question, voice vs typed, and timing medians (founder's notes).
- A public debrief to members titled "what we built, what broke, and what AI changed" (founder's notes).
- A preparation note that scores the offer against his own checklist and lists who to call on and why (founder's notes).

**Reconcile revenue to one number.** Three sources reported three different Build Lab buyer counts (founder's notes). Instrument so you can tell which email made money: the customer count was several times the checkout_started count (founder's notes).

**Promote lessons to playbooks** at three or more data points (founder's notes).

## 4. Lessons and failure patterns (what broke)

1. **Partner-fronted promise vs who actually teaches.** All 16 emails were first-person from the promotion partner, including "I'll see you there". Five minutes into Day 1: "I thought [the promotion partner] was presenting this." Lanny: "I'd probably be like, oh my god, I've been duped." The rule existed in the playbook and was violated in the shipped emails (founder's notes, "Verified ground truth"). **Skill rule:** the invitation must name who teaches live.
2. **Proof inflation.** Email 9 turned a paid-program member's account (she raised her price and closed a five-figure sum within two weeks) into "two conversations… a [five-figure] check". She was also a paid-program member (founder's notes). **Skill rule:** classify proof first, then use the speaker's own words plus an honest qualifier: "Are you going to make [that] because you completed these two steps? No. Probably not."
3. **Unkept or contradictory promises in pre-event emails.** Email 5 said "something is coming" and there is no evidence it shipped. Email 6 said "not a sales call" for calls routed toward enrollment. The promo said "25 minutes" when the median was about 49 (founder's notes).
4. **Reminder misfires on consecutive days.** The Day 2 "we're live" email fired ~4 hours early, twice; Day 3 opened at about 60% of its eventual room. "I said, hey, let's make sure it's right for day 3… And it happened again" (founder's notes). **Fix:** "A tested schedule with a dry-run 24h before" (founder's notes).
5. **No Day-1-night or Day-2-night email** (founder's notes).
6. **Show-up gates create friction.** The live access code blocked named attendees on camera and was removed live about 90 minutes in. A 20-hour delay on a paid deliverable needed manual overrides (founder's notes).
7. **Untested live tech.**
   - First-ever breakout rooms cost about 45 minutes: "I have trauma. I need EMDR because of those breakout rooms" (founder's notes).
   - Email-in-URL caused real data loss when links were forwarded (founder's notes).
   - The app was self-hosted on office internet and went down on Day 2 (founder's notes).
   - A live song app returned silence at a few dozen concurrent users, and Lanny told the room "Please don't buy" the low-priced video (founder's notes).
8. **Checkout broken at peak intent at two consecutive camps,** and nobody could buy in September. Feb: "duplicate order forms, a broken order bump". Jul: Stripe "you may only specify one of these parameters, customer or customer_email". The warning sign on Day 2 was ignored. Sep: "I built a door… and forgot to unlock it" (founder's notes).
9. **No structured close; confused offers.**
   - July Day 3 had "no deadline, no recap… no call to action".
   - The hundred-app bonus read as bloat: "what do we need all of that for?" / "You probably don't."
   - In September people didn't know what Liftoff was ("I don't know exactly what's in it", Lanny, Day 2). One attendee asked to be pitched the Build Room, which undercut the core offer.
   - Plans that punish, and two contradictory guarantees (founder's notes).
10. **Slowing down ready buyers** by forcing them through every phase before they could pay (founder's notes).
11. **Follow-up engine switched off.** None of the ten workflows was published, a couple of thousand enrollments sat frozen, and approval was stale on a reply that had already arrived. "The entire post-event conversion window, spent" (founder's notes). Only buyers got fast follow-up.
12. **Single-point-of-failure sending.** The partner controlled the sending and the reminders ("I didn't handle these emails, [the partner's] team did"). Earlier, CRM import gaps left people off sequences and invites landed in spam (founder's notes, "Sending is the single point of failure").
13. **Post-purchase access failures** are "the most commercially expensive pattern": replay and Zoom links hand-delivered, wrong ZIPs, stuck deployments (founder's notes).
14. **Undocumented prices and terms.** "Eight distinct price/term shapes were quoted to customers in fourteen days"; an approved plan went 11+ days without a payment link (founder's notes).
15. **No program calendar or replay index.** Dates were inferred from inbound email ("Build Lab started Monday 2026-08-10", inferred). The Sep 10–12 bootcamp was "recorded nowhere canonical" (founder's notes).
16. **Proof-free follow-up emails.** "Zero proof across all 32 emails… while a verification-audited proof bank sits unused" (founder's notes).
17. **Self-defeating subject lines** such as "A clear no is allowed" and "I'll leave this with you" (founder's notes).
18. **Seminar-era lessons.**
    - Don't send people to lunch with an unfilled application.
    - "Some negatives backfire": a "don't trust the internet / BBB" video cost sales.
    - Back-table staff chatting devalues the speaker.
    - Never run the back-table rush mid-presentation.
    - Traffic concentration on one channel, with ad learning reset per city, is a risk (founder's notes).
19. **What worked (protect).** Pre-event self-serve tool; first-party app (credited with the retention gain); personalized outputs (a named attendee's own project); the one-dollar commitment device; the AI-Lanny Day-3 video; radical transparency; community volunteers; the founder as instructor; the age-as-advantage frame (founder's notes).

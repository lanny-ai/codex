# Webinar Builder: outside research (2026-11-11)

Short pass for the Webinar Builder skill. Strength tags: **[strong]** primary source (regulator, standard, vendor docs), **[medium]** reputable industry report or institutional guide, **[weak]** blog or anecdote. The session's network blocked direct fetches from on24.com, goto.com, ftc.gov and zoom.com, so these figures come from indexed extracts of those pages; items marked *verify* should be checked against the primary before they are quoted to members.

## 1. Benchmarks

- **Registration to attendance, counting replays.** ON24 2026 report (2025 data): 60% registrant-to-attendee conversion, with on-demand viewers about half of all attendees; 2025 report (2024 data): 57%, on-demand 45%. Average engagement 49 minutes both years. Enterprise B2B sample, so high for small business. on24.com/resources/asset/webinar-benchmarks-report **[medium]** *verify*
- **Live-only show-up is about a third to a half.** Livestorm 2026 report: 47.7% average show-up across 33,786 sessions in 2025 **[medium]**. Goldcast 2025 B2B report: 33% average attendance, 29 minutes average watch time, recommends 45–60 minutes **[medium]**. Zoom's blog quotes Univid at about 49% live and 57% with replays **[medium]**.
- **Reminder timing.** WebinarGeek benchmark (2024 data): one reminder 1 hour before the start gave 50.7% live attendance against 24.6% when the reminder went at the start time **[medium]**. The 24-hour, 1-hour and "starting now" sequence is common practice **[weak]**; no data found for a 15-minute reminder.
- **Days and times conflict across sources** (GoTo: Wednesday and Thursday 10–11 a.m.; a widely repeated "Thursday 64%" figure could not be traced; BigMarker via Zoom: 11 a.m.–2 p.m.; Goldcast 2025 favours Friday). Treat as something each presenter tests.
- **Length preference.** A WorkCast survey cited by Zoom: 41% of attendees prefer 30 minutes, 44% prefer 45 minutes **[weak/medium]**.
- **Gaps.** No published benchmark for small-business or coaching sales webinars; none from Demio or WebinarJam. Do not invent coaching-specific numbers.

## 2. Rules (United States; not legal advice)

- **FTC Act Section 5 (law)** bans deceptive acts and practices. No FTC rule names "simulated live" webinars and no case was found resting on a recording shown as live, but a false "live" claim, an invented attendee count or seeded chat is a material misrepresentation under Section 5.
- **FTC staff report "Bringing Dark Patterns to Light" (September 2022, guidance)** names baseless countdown timers that reset or vanish, and false scarcity, as deceptive **[strong for the position]**.
- **Consumer Reviews and Testimonials Rule, 16 CFR 465 (law, in force 21 October 2024, civil penalties)** bans fake or AI-generated testimonials and testimonials that misrepresent the person's experience; §465.8 bans buying or selling fake indicators of social influence. 89 FR 68034 **[strong]**
- **Endorsement Guides, 16 CFR 255 (guidance, revised 26 July 2023)**: testimonials reflect honest experience; a testimonial showing atypical results implies they are typical; material connections disclosed clearly. 88 FR 48092 **[strong]**
- **Business Opportunity Rule, 16 CFR 437 (law)**; the January 2025 proposal to extend it to business coaching and require substantiation of earnings claims **is still only proposed** as of 2026 **[strong for the proposal; status from a weak secondary report]** *verify*
- **Enforcement 2024–2026** keeps targeting earnings claims: Operation AI Comply (from September 2024, continuing), and 2025 cases against sellers of business-opportunity and AI "passive income" programs with industry bans **[medium/strong]**.
- **Click to Cancel (the Negative Option Rule) is not in force**: vacated by the Eighth Circuit on 8 July 2025; ROSCA and state auto-renewal laws still apply **[medium]** *verify*

## 3. Zoom and GoHighLevel

- **Zoom Webinars** is an add-on to a paid licence, sold by attendee tier from 500; third-party prices conflict, so quote the member's own account page **[weak]**. **Zoom Meetings**: Pro holds 100 participants, Business 300; a Large Meetings add-on raises one meeting to 500 or 1,000 **[medium]**.
- **Webinar features**: Q&A with upvoting and anonymous questions, polls, registration with custom questions, a practice session seen only by host and panelists **[medium]**.
- **Exports**: cloud recording gives MP4, M4A audio, an "Audio Transcript" .VTT (when "Create audio transcript" is on) and the chat as .TXT (when "Save chat" is on); some accounts delete recordings after 30 days, so download promptly **[medium]**.
- **Captions**: automatic captions free on all Meetings and Webinars accounts since October 2021 **[strong]**; a report that caption saving ended in May 2026 is unverified.
- **GoHighLevel** builds webinar funnels (registration pages, reminders, workflows) but does not broadcast live video; the broadcast runs on Zoom or another tool. Registering people into a Zoom Webinar from GoHighLevel needs Zapier or Make per an open feature request **[medium]**; WebinarJam connects through Zapier or Integrately **[weak]**.

## 4. Accessibility for older viewers

- **WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.3 (AA)**: at least 4.5:1 contrast for normal text, 3:1 for large text (18pt, or 14pt bold) **[strong]**. SC 1.2.2 and 1.2.4: captions for recorded and live media **[strong]**.
- **Slide type**: no standards body sets a point size; university accessibility offices converge on 24pt minimum body text and 32pt or more for titles **[medium]**.
- **Older users (W3C WAI)**: reduced contrast sensitivity, colour perception and near focus; an 80-year-old has about 80% less contrast sensitivity than a 20-year-old, decline starting in the mid-40s **[strong, archival]**. NIA's older-adult guidance: sans serif, high contrast **[strong but dated]**.

## Design implications taken into the skill

1. Two show-up figures, never one: live attendance about a third to a half of registrants; the 60% figure only when replay viewers are counted, and labelled B2B.
2. Reminders at 24 hours, 1 hour (best evidenced) and "starting now" are the Event Launcher's; the skill points there.
3. A 60-minute default with the strongest material in the first 30 minutes; the offer before the typical watch-time drop and restated once; a replay with a real expiry.
4. The check script blocks a "live" claim on a recording, timers or scarcity that are not real, invented counts, seeded chat, testimonials without an ID and class, and earnings figures without the typical result.
5. Every legal line labelled law, guidance, proposed or vacated, with "not legal advice".
6. Slide floors set above the institutional minimum for older eyes on laptops: body 28pt, titles 40pt, contrast 4.5:1 minimum and 7:1 as the target.
7. Zoom run: practice session, automatic captions on and announced, the audio transcript and chat saved and downloaded promptly so the review can read them.
8. GoHighLevel is the page and CRM layer; Zoom is the broadcast.
